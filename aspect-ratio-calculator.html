Frequently Asked Questions

What is aspect ratio?

Aspect ratio is the proportional relationship between an image's width and height, expressed as W:H (e.g., 16:9).

What is 16:9 in pixels?

Common 16:9 resolutions: 1280×720 (HD), 1920×1080 (Full HD), 2560×1440 (QHD), 3840×2160 (4K). Use the calculator to find any custom size.

What aspect ratio is best for YouTube?

YouTube recommends 16:9 (1920×1080 Full HD). Shorts use 9:16 (1080×1920). Thumbnails are 1280×720.

What aspect ratio is best for Instagram?

1:1 square (1080×1080), 4:5 portrait (1080×1350), or 16:9 landscape (1080×608). Reels use 9:16 (1080×1920).

📚 Educational Content — Deep Dive

Aspect ratio is one of the most fundamental concepts in visual media. The formula to maintain aspect ratio is simple: New Height = (Original Height / Original Width) × New Width. Getting this wrong results in stretched or squished images that look unprofessional. This calculator automates the math instantly, letting designers and video editors focus on creative work rather than arithmetic.
