Protect Your Privacy with One Click

Every time you take a photo with your smartphone, it records hidden data called **EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format)**. This data often includes your exact GPS coordinates, the date and time of the photo, and the specific device used. Sharing these photos online means sharing your private location.

💎 Lossless & No Re-encoding

Unlike other tools that "save as" a new image (which loses quality), our tool performs **Binary Surgery**. We surgically remove the metadata segments from the file buffer, keeping every single pixel exactly as it was original.

🔒 100% Client-Side

Your photos are **never uploaded to any server**. The entire scanning and scrubbing process happens locally on your computer or phone. This is the only way to ensure 100% privacy for sensitive photos.

What metadata is removed?

  • GPS Location: Latitude, Longitude, and Altitude where the photo was taken.
  • Device Info: Smartphone model, camera maker, lens specs, and serial numbers.
  • Software: Editing apps used (e.g., Photoshop, Lightroom) and OS versions.
  • Date/Time: Absolute timestamp of when the shutter was pressed.
  • User Data: Copyright info, owner name, and comments.
