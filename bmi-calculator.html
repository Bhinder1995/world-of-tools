What is the BMI Calculator?

The WorldOfTools BMI Calculator is a free, private, instant tool that calculates your Body Mass Index (BMI) using WHO and CDC standard guidelines. Enter your height and weight in metric (kg/cm) or imperial (lbs/ft/in) units and get your BMI value, health category, healthy weight range, ideal weight, and personalised guidance — all in your browser with zero data storage.

BMI is a widely used screening measure to assess weight-related health risks in adults aged 18 and above. It is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared (kg/m²). While it has limitations (it doesn't distinguish muscle from fat), it remains a valuable first-line health indicator.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI range for adults?

According to WHO and CDC guidelines, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is healthy for adults. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30 or above is obese. These categories apply to adults aged 18 and above, and use the same ranges for men and women.

Is BMI accurate for Indians and Asians?

Some research suggests Asian populations have higher cardiometabolic risks at lower BMI values. The WHO proposes a lower overweight cut-off of 23.0 for Asian populations. This tool uses the standard international WHO ranges — consult a healthcare professional for personalised Asian BMI guidance.

What is the BMI formula?

Metric: BMI = weight(kg) ÷ height²(m²). Imperial: BMI = 703 × weight(lbs) ÷ height²(inches²). For example: 70kg at 175cm = 70 ÷ (1.75²) = 70 ÷ 3.0625 = 22.9.

Why might BMI be inaccurate for athletes?

BMI doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat. A professional athlete may have a "Overweight" or "Obese" BMI due to high muscle mass, despite having very low body fat. For athletes, body fat percentage and waist circumference are better health indicators.

How do I calculate ideal weight from BMI?

Ideal weight = target BMI × height²(m²). A common target is BMI 22 (midpoint of healthy range). This tool shows this automatically — for a person 175cm tall, ideal weight = 22 × 1.75² = 67.4 kg.

Is my weight and height data private?

Yes. All BMI calculations run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your weight and height are never uploaded to any server, never stored in any database, and never shared with anyone.
