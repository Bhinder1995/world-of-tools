What is Aspect Ratio?

Aspect ratio is the proportional relationship between the width and height of an image or video screen. It is expressed as two numbers separated by a colon, like **16:9** (Widescreen) or **1:1** (Square).

Popular Ratios

  • 16:9: Standard for YouTube, TV, and monitors.
  • 9:16: Perfect for Reels, TikToks, and Phone Stories.
  • 1:1: Classic Instagram square posts.
  • 4:5: Instagram portraitposts for maximum feed space.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is it important to maintain aspect ratio?
Scaling an image without maintaining its ratio leads to stretching or squashing, making your content look unprofessional and distorted.
What is 4K resolution aspect ratio?
True 4K (3840×2160) is exactly 16:9. Our calculator allows you to find proportional resolutions for any display.
