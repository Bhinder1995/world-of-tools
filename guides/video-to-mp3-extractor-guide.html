Frequently Asked Questions

What is 320kbps?

Bitrate refers to the amount of data processed per second. 320kbps is the highest standard quality for MP3 files, offering crystal-clear sound that is indistinguishable from CD quality.

Can I extract audio from YouTube videos?

Yes, if you have the video file on your device. Simply upload it to our tool. We do not support direct URL fetching to comply with privacy and safety standards.

Is there a length limit?

Technically no, but very long videos (over 1 hour) might take a few minutes to process depending on your computer's CPU speed.
