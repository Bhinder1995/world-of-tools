What is a Favicon?

A favicon (short for "favorite icon") is a small icon displayed next to your website title in browser tabs, bookmarks, and search results. It helps users identify your site quickly and adds a layer of professionalism to your brand.

Standard Favicon Sizes

  • 16x16: Standard for browser tabs.
  • 32x32: Used for taskbar shortcuts and desktop icons.
  • 180x180: Optimized for Apple devices (Touch Icons).
  • 512x512: Higher resolution for Android and PWA splash screens.
