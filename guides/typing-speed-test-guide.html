What is WPM?

WPM stands for **Words Per Minute**. It is the standard measure of typing speed. A "word" is standardized as five characters or keystrokes (including spaces). So, if you type 250 characters in one minute, your speed is 50 WPM.

Typing Speed Benchmarks

  • Beginner: 10–30 WPM
  • Average: 35–45 WPM (Office work standard)
  • Intermediate: 50–70 WPM
  • Advanced: 75–100 WPM (Professional typists)
  • Master: 120+ WPM

Frequently Asked Questions

How is accuracy calculated?
Accuracy is the percentage of correct keystrokes out of total keystrokes. For example, if you type 100 characters and 5 are wrong, your accuracy is 95%.
How can I improve my typing speed?
Practice touch typing (using all 10 fingers without looking at the keys). Use our 5-minute test daily to build muscle memory and focus on accuracy first — speed follows accuracy naturally.
What is a good WPM for a job?
Most office jobs require at least 40 WPM. Data entry and transcription roles often require 60–80 WPM with high accuracy.
