💡 Pro Tip: The Power of Tenure

While a 20-year home loan has a lower EMI than a 15-year one, you will pay significantly more in total interest. Always compare "Total Payment" to see exactly how much extra interest the longer tenure adds to your burden.

Key metrics to look For

  • Monthly EMI: This is the amount you pay every month. Ensure this fits comfortably within your budget.
  • Total Interest Paid: This shows how much you're paying the bank in addition to the principal. Reducing this is the goal of an efficient loan.
  • Total Payment: This is the most important metric. It's the sum of the principal, all interest, and any processing fees. This is the true cost of your loan.
  • Amortization Schedule: View the year-by-year breakdown to see how your balance reduces over time.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there a green highlight on one of my loans?

The green highlight identifies the "Winner" — the loan with the lowest total repayment amount. This is generally the best financial deal, provided the EMI fits your budget.

Can I compare a home loan against a personal loan?

Yes. You can use different loan amounts, tenures, and rates in each slot to compare entirely different scenarios, like taking a loan or liquidating an investment instead.

What is an Amortization Schedule?

It's a table showing each payment over the life of the loan. In the early years, most of your EMI goes toward interest rather than principal. Seeing this helps you plan for early repayments.
