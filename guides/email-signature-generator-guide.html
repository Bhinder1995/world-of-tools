Why Use a Professional Signature?

An email signature is more than just your name; it's a digital business card. A well-designed signature builds trust, provides essential contact info, and reinforces your professional brand with every email you send.

What to Include

  • Full Name & Title: Who you are and what you do.
  • Contact Details: Phone, email, and website.
  • Social Links: LinkedIn is essential for professionals.
  • Professional Photo/Logo: Increases recognition and trust.

How to Install Your Signature

For Gmail users:

  1. Generate your signature and click **Copy HTML**.
  2. Open Gmail and go to **Settings > See all settings**.
  3. In the **General** tab, scroll down to **Signature**.
  4. Click **Create New**, then paste the copied signature into the box.
  5. Scroll down and click **Save Changes**.

For Outlook users:

  1. Click **Copy HTML** in our generator.
  2. Open Outlook and go to **File > Options > Mail**.
  3. Click **Signatures**, then **New**.
  4. In the "Edit signature" box, paste your new signature.
  5. Click **OK** to save.
