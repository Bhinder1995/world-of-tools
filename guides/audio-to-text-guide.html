How to Transcribe Audio to Text (Real-time)

  1. Select Your Language: Use the dropdown menu to choose from 30+ supported languages, including English (Global), Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and more.
  2. Grant Microphone Access: Click the 🎙️ mic button. Your browser will ask for permission to use your microphone; click "Allow".
  3. Start Speaking: Begin talking at a moderate pace. The transcript will appear in the text box in real-time as you speak.
  4. Continuous Mode: Keep the "Continuous Mode" checked if you are dictating a long passage or a multi-person meeting.
  5. Download/Copy: Once finished, click the ⏹️ button to stop. You can then copy the text to your clipboard or download it as a .txt file.

💡 Pro Tip: Transcribing Audio Files

While the tool primarily uses your microphone, you can transcribe pre-recorded audio files easily. Simply play the audio file (or YouTube video) through your device's speakers while the microphone tool is recording. For better results on PC/Mac, use "System Audio" as your microphone input source in your OS settings.

Best Practices for High Accuracy

To get 95%+ accuracy in your transcriptions, follow these tips:

  • Use a Headset: A dedicated microphone or headset reduces background echo and provides clearer audio levels.
  • Minimize Background Noise: Fan noise, traffic, or other people speaking can confuse the recognition engine.
  • Speak Clearly: Avoid mumbling and try to maintain a consistent volume throughout your session.

There is no word limit! As long as your browser tab remains active and you have "Continuous Mode" enabled, you can transcribe for hours.

Do I need to pay for Indian language transcription?

No. All languages, including Hindi, Tamil, and Bengali, are available for free with no usage limits.
