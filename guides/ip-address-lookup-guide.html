What is an IP Address?

An IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique numerical label assigned to every device connected to a computer network. Think of it as your digital home address on the internet, allowing servers and websites to know where to send data.

What Details Can You Find?

  • Public IP Address: Your unique ID on the open web.
  • Location: Your city, region, and country (based on IP).
  • ISP: The name of your Internet Service Provider.
  • Timezone: Your current local time and zone offset.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my location slightly off?
IP geolocation shows the location of your ISP's nearest data center or server hub, not your exact physical home address. This provides a layer of privacy for users.
IPv4 vs IPv6 — what's the difference?
IPv4 is the older, standard format (e.g., 172.67.12.3). IPv6 is the new, longer format designed to accommodate the growing number of devices on the internet. Our tool supports both.
