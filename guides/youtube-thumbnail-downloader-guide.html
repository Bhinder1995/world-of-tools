Understanding YouTube's Image Architecture

When you upload a video, YouTube automatically generates several versions of your thumbnail to serve different devices and bandwidth speeds. Our tool allows you to access all of them:

  • HD (1280x720): The highest quality available. Perfect for desktop viewing and design references. These are often labeled as "maxresdefault" in the source code.
  • SD (640x480): Standard definition. Used for older devices or smaller embeds.
  • HQ (480x360): High quality for mobile previews.
  • MQ (320x180): Medium quality. Used for the recommended videos sidebar.
