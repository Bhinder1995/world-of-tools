Best Practices for Bio Fonts

1. Don't Over-Stylize

While fancy fonts look great, using them for the entire bio can make it hard to read. Use stylistic fonts for your **Name** or **Call to Action**, but keep your main details in a standard readable font.

2. Screen Reader Accessibility

Unicode fonts are symbols. While they look like letters, some screen readers for the visually impaired might read them as "Mathematical Bold Script A." Use them sparingly to stay inclusive.

3. Mix & Match

Combine a bold serif for your title with a light script for your emoji descriptions. Our tool lets you copy multiple styles sequentially.
