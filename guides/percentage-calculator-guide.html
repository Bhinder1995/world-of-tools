The Anatomy of a Percentage

To understand the math, remember the core formula:
Part / Whole = Percentage / 100

This simple ratio is the foundation of all calculations. Whether you are finding the part, the whole, or the percentage, you are just solving for one missing variable in this equation. Our tool does this rearrangement automatically for you in milliseconds.

💡 Pro Tip: Percentage vs. Percentage Points

If an interest rate increases from 5% to 7%, it has increased by 2 percentage points, but it has actually increased by 40% relative to its original value! Always be clear which metric you are discussing in professional reports.
