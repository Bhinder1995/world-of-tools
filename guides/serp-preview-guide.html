What is SERP?

SERP stands for **Search Engine Results Page**. It is the page Google shows when you search for something. Optimizing your result snippet (title and description) is crucial for standing out among competitors and driving clicks to your site.

SEO Best Practices

  • Title Length: Keep it between 50–60 characters to avoid being cut off.
  • Meta Description: Aim for 140–160 characters with a clear call to action.
  • Keywords: Place your primary keyword near the start of the title.
  • Brand Name: Include your brand name at the end of the title if space permits.

How to Use the SERP Preview Tool

  1. Enter Page Title: Watch the live score — green means perfect length, red means it will be truncated.
  2. Add Meta Description: Use persuasive language. The preview box updates instantly to show the Google mock-up.
  3. Input URL: See the breadcrumb path exactly as users will see it.
  4. Check Mobile: Toggle the mobile mock-up to ensure your snippet looks great on smartphones too.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does meta description affect rankings?
Not directly. However, a great meta description increases CTR. Google notices more people clicking your result, which can lead to higher rankings over time.
Why does Google sometimes change my title?
Google may rewrite your title if they feel a different phrase better matches the user's specific search query. Using this tool helps you provide the "best" suggestion for them.
