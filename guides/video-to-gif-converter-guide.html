Why GIFs are Better than Videos?

In the age of social media, **GIFs** are the universal language of emotion and communication. They are lightweight, loop indefinitely, and most importantly—autostart on almost every platform. Whether for a Discord reaction, a Reddit post, or a professional product walk-through, a high-quality GIF is invaluable.

Our **Video to GIF Converter** uses the legendary FFmpeg engine via WebAssembly, giving you desktop-grade power directly in your browser without watermarks or file uploads.

Steps to Create the Perfect GIF

  1. Selection: Upload your MP4, MOV, or WEBM. We support high-resolution inputs.
  2. Trimming: Use the Start Time and Duration inputs to select the exact highlight. Frame-perfect precision is key.
  3. FPS (Frames Per Second): Choose 15-20 FPS for social media. For buttery smooth animations, go up to 30 FPS.
  4. Resolution Scailing: Downscale to 480p or 360p if you need to stay under the 8MB limit for apps like Discord or Telegram.
  5. Optimization: Our tool automatically applies advanced dithering and palette generation for color-rich GIFs.

Best Practice: Balance Quality vs. Size

One common mistake is making GIFs too large. A 30 FPS, 1080p GIF will easily exceed 50MB. We recommend 15 FPS and 480p resolution for the perfect balance of clarity and file size. Most reaction GIFs are less than 5 second in duration.
