Inclusive vs. Exclusive GST: What's the Difference?

One of the most common points of confusion in taxation is the difference between GST inclusive and exclusive pricing. Understanding this distinction is vital for accurate pricing and budgeting.

  • GST Exclusive: This is the base price of a product or service before the tax is added. For example, if a consultant charges $1,000 "plus GST," the total amount paid will be higher than $1,000.
  • GST Inclusive: This is the final price that includes the tax. If a consumer buys a laptop for $1,200 "GST inclusive," the tax is already baked into that $1,200 sticker price.

Our tool allows you to toggle between these two modes instantly. If you have the total price and want to find the original amount, use the "Remove GST" option. If you have the base cost and need the final price, use the "Add GST" option.

📊 The Math Behind the GST Hook

To calculate GST manually:
Adding GST: Total = Base Amount + (Base Amount * GST% / 100)
Removing GST: GST Amount = Total - (Total / (1 + (GST% / 100)))
