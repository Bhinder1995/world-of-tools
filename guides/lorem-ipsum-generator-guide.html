The Psychology of Placeholder Text

Why not just use "Sample Text" over and over? Or copy a news article? There are three main psychological reasons designers use Lorem Ipsum:

  • Varying Word Lengths: Real English sentences have a mix of long and short words. Repetitive "Sample Text" creates a "blocky" look that doesn't reflect how a final page will actually feel.
  • Reduced Cognitive Load: If you use recognizable text, the human brain will naturally try to read it. This distracts the viewer from the design elements like spacing, color, and font choice.
  • Layout Neutrality: It ensures that a client doesn't get hung up on a specific word choice or a typo, allowing for constructive feedback on the UI/UX architecture.

The History: From Cicero to the Digital Age

Lorem Ipsum comes from sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (The Extremes of Good and Evil) by Cicero, written in 45 BC. It was rediscovered in the 1960s when Letraset sheets featuring the text were used by advertising agencies, and it later became part of the default setup for word processors like PageMaker and Microsoft Word.

🚀 Designer Workflow: When to Stop Using It
