Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tool handle months with different days?

Our algorithm uses the "Borrowing" method similar to manual subtraction but optimized for dates. If the current day is less than the birth day, it "borrows" the number of days in the previous month to ensure a seamless calculation of months and remaining days.

Can I calculate my age on a future date?

Yes! Simply change the "Calculate age on" field from today's date to any future date. This is great for planning for milestones or retirement.

Is my date of birth stored on your servers?

Absolutely not. WorldOfTools is a privacy-first platform. All logic is executed in your client-side browser using JavaScript. No data is transmitted to our backend.

Why does my age on other sites differ by a day?

Some tools use simplified versions of "30 days per month" or "365 days per year." We use the actual days in each month and year, making our result the most accurate possible representation of your chronological age.

Which day of the week was I born on?

The Age Calculator automatically detects and displays the day of the week (e.g., Monday, Saturday) you were born on in the "Birthday Intelligence" section.
