Calculating Semester GPA (SGPA)

Your Semester GPA is the weighted average of your grade points across all subjects in a single semester. To calculate it:

  1. Enter Subject Names: List the subjects you took during the semester.
  2. Select Grade Points: Choose the letter grade or numeric points (e.g., 10 for 'O', 9 for 'A+') you received.
  3. Input Credits: Enter the credit hours assigned to each subject (e.g., 3, 4, or 2).
  4. Calculate: The tool uses the formula: Σ(Grade Points × Credits) ÷ Σ(Credits) to find your SGPA.

🏛️ University-Specific Percentage Formulas

While the generic formula is CGPA × 9.5 , many universities have unique methods:

  • VTU: (CGPA − 0.75) × 10
  • Mumbai University: CGPA × 7.1 + 11
  • Anna University: CGPA × 10 − 7.5
  • UPTU / AKTU: CGPA × 9.5
  • KTU (Kerala): CGPA × 10 − 5

Calculating Cumulative CGPA

CGPA is the combined average of all your semesters. Our tool makes this easy:

  1. Add Semesters: Use the "Add Semester" button for each term completed.
  2. Enter SGPA and Credits: Input your GPA for each semester and the total credits for that term.
  3. Final Result: The tool calculates your weighted cumulative average across your entire degree.

Converting CGPA to Percentage (%)

For job applications, placements, and government exams, you often need to submit your marks in percentage. Usually, the formula followed is the CBSE/AICTE standard:

  • Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
  • For example, if your CGPA is 8.0, your percentage is 8.0 × 9.5 = 76%.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why do universities multiply CGPA by 9.5?

The 9.5 multiplier is the standard set by CBSE and AICTE to approximate percentage from a 10-point scale, accounting for the fact that scoring 100% is extremely rare in weighted grading.

What is a good CGPA for campus placements?

A CGPA of 6.5 or above is generally required by major IT firms like TCS and Infosys. For premium product-based companies or top-tier MNCs, a CGPA of 7.5 or 8.0+ is often requested.

Does this tool work for 4-point GPA (US Scale)?

Yes. You can switch the grading scale to "4-point" in the SGPA tab to calculate GPA for international university standards.
