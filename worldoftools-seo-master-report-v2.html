✅ Why these hooks beat "Free"

🔒 Private / No Upload — SmallSEOTools, JSONLint, JSONFormatter all upload your data. You don't. Say it.

✨ No Watermark — Every image/video compressor competitor adds a watermark on free tier. Huge pain point users actively search to avoid.

⚡ No Signup — Users are trained to expect "free but you must register." No signup = instant friction removal.

🇮🇳 India-First — No global competitor targets "GST 2026–27", "PPF FY26", "CGPA DU formula." Low competition, high intent.

♾️ No Limits — Competitors have daily limits on free tier. If you have none, say it — it's a massive differentiator.

⭐ Unique — LinkedIn Creator Suite has zero competitors. Don't call it "free" — call it what it is: the only one.
❌ When NOT to use "Free" in title

— When a privacy hook is stronger (developer tools)
— When "no watermark" is the real selling point
— When the tool is unique — "free" undersells it
— When "no signup" is more compelling than price
— When "India" geo-modifier is more relevant
— When competitors are also free — price parity = no advantage

✅ Always put "free" in the meta description — it helps CTR there without wasting title space on a word all competitors also use.
