Sentence Case vs. Title Case: Which to Use?

Choosing the right letter case is crucial for professional copywriting and readability. Here's a quick guide:

Sentence Case

Ideal for blog posts and emails. It flows naturally and is easiest for the human eye to read long-form content.

Title Case

Used for headlines and titles. It grabs attention and makes your section headers stand out.
