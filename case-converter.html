Frequently Asked Questions

What is Title Case?

Every major word is capitalized. Example: "the quick brown fox" becomes "The Quick Brown Fox".

What is Sentence case?

Only the first letter of the sentence is capitalized. Like normal writing.

What is camelCase?

Words joined with no spaces, each word capitalized except the first. E.g. myVariableName.

What is snake_case?

Words joined with underscores, all lowercase. E.g. my_variable_name.

Is text stored?

No. All conversion happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
