Frequently Asked Questions

What is camelCase?

camelCase capitalizes the first letter of each word except the first: `myVariableName`. Widely used in JavaScript, Java, and JSON.

What is snake_case?

Words separated by underscores, all lowercase: `my_variable_name`. Common in Python, SQL, and Ruby.

What is kebab-case?

Words separated by hyphens, all lowercase: `my-variable-name`. Standard for CSS class names and URL slugs.

What is PascalCase?

First letter of every word capitalized, no separators: `MyClassName`. Used for class names in C#, Java, and TypeScript.
