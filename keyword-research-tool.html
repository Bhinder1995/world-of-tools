What is a Keyword Research Tool?

The Keyword Research Tool by WorldOfTools helps bloggers, SEO professionals, and content creators discover the exact search terms their target audience uses on Google. Instead of guessing, you get structured keyword ideas organised by intent, format, and competition level — all without any signup or paid subscription.

How to Do Keyword Research for SEO in India

Start with a broad seed keyword in the Keyword Ideas tab. Use the Long-Tail Finder for low-competition phrases, then switch to Questions to capture featured snippet opportunities. Finally, use Competitor Angles to discover what alternative and review searches you can target.

How to Get Competitor Traffic Insights?

It is crucial to understand how top competitors get their traffic. Using the Competitor Angles tab, you can pinpoint the comparative phrases users type when evaluating products or services in your niche (e.g. 'alternative to', 'versus'). This helps you perform a fundamental keyword gap analysis to create content pages that hijack competitor traffic—an essential SEO keyword finder strategy used by professionals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a keyword "low competition"?

Low-competition keywords typically have 3+ words (long-tail), are question-based, or are hyper-specific. Our tool labels these as Low difficulty.

How is keyword difficulty calculated here?

Difficulty is estimated based on keyword length (more words = lower difficulty), presence of commercial modifiers, and keyword specificity — all calculated locally in your browser.

Can I export keywords to Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. Click "Export CSV" after generating results. The file opens directly in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app.

Is my keyword data stored on your servers?

No. This tool is fully client-side. All keyword generation runs in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or shared.
