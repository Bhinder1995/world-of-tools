Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between formats?

CODE128 is for general alphabetic/numeric data, while EAN-13 and UPC are specifically for retail products used by POS systems globally.

Can I use barcodes for inventory?

Yes, CODE128 and CODE39 are excellent for internal inventory tracking and management systems.
