About This Speech-to-Text Tool

This free audio to text converter leverages the W3C Web Speech API, built directly into modern browsers like Chrome, Edge, and Safari. Unlike traditional transcription software requiring installation or paid subscriptions, this tool works entirely within your browser tab — no software download, no account, no time limit.

Speech recognition accuracy has improved dramatically thanks to deep learning. Google's and Apple's recognition engines now handle accents, mixed-language speech (like Hinglish), and natural spoken language with over 90% accuracy in quiet environments. Ideal for students taking dictation notes, content writers drafting articles, professionals transcribing meetings, and anyone who types slowly but speaks fast.

Unlike Otter.ai, Rev, or Descript — which all heavily limit free usage — WorldOfTools provides this tool with zero paywalls. Your transcribed text is never stored on our servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this audio to text converter really free?

Yes, completely free — no account, no subscription, no hidden fees. The tool uses the browser's native Web Speech API which is available in Chrome, Edge, and Safari at no cost.

What languages does this speech to text tool support?

30+ languages including all major Indian regional languages: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, and Urdu — plus English (India, US, UK), Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Russian, and Italian.

How accurate is the transcription?

For clear speech in a quiet environment, accuracy is typically 90–95% for English. Indian language accuracy is improving rapidly. Speaking slowly and clearly, reducing background noise, and using a quality microphone significantly improves results.

Does this work offline?

Chrome's Web Speech API requires an internet connection as it sends audio to Google's speech servers inside your browser. Safari on newer Apple devices supports fully on-device recognition and can work offline.

Can I use this to transcribe YouTube videos or meetings?

Yes! Play the video audio through your speakers while the microphone is recording. For Zoom or Google Meet, configure your OS audio settings to route system audio through your microphone input for internal audio capture.

Is my audio data stored on WorldOfTools servers?

No. The transcribed text output is generated in your browser and never stored on WorldOfTools servers. The audio is processed by your browser's built-in speech recognition engine (Google's in Chrome, Apple's in Safari).
