❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this audio to text converter really free?

Yes, completely free — no account, no subscription, no hidden fees. The tool uses the browser's native Web Speech API.

What languages does this speech to text tool support?

30+ languages including all major Indian regional languages: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, and Urdu.

Does this work offline?

Chrome's Web Speech API requires an internet connection as it processes speech server-side within Google's infrastructure. However, Safari on newer Apple devices supports on-device recognition.

How accurate is the transcription?

For clear speech in a quiet environment, accuracy is typically 90–95% for English. Indian language accuracy is improving rapidly. Speaking slowly and clearly improves results significantly.

Can I use this to transcribe YouTube videos or meetings?

Yes! Play the video audio through your speakers while recording. For meetings on Zoom/Google Meet, configure system audio to route through your microphone input.
