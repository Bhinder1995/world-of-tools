Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal keyword density for SEO?

Most SEO experts recommend 1–3% for a primary keyword. Above 3% risks being flagged as keyword stuffing. Focus on natural, topic-relevant writing over mechanical density targets.

Does keyword density still affect Google rankings?

Google uses semantic analysis and doesn't rely on exact density numbers. However, unnaturally high repetition (keyword stuffing) can trigger algorithmic penalties. Write for humans first.

What is keyword stuffing?

Keyword stuffing is the excessive, unnatural repetition of a keyword to manipulate search rankings. Example: "Buy cheap shoes. Our cheap shoes store sells cheap shoes." This is a Google guideline violation.

How do I check my keyword density?

Paste your content here, enter your target keyword in the focus keyword field, and see its count, density %, and position in the frequency table.

Should I use exact match keywords or variations?

Use semantic variations (LSI keywords) rather than repeating the exact same phrase. Google understands topically related terms and rewards comprehensive, varied coverage.
