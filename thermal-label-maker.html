What is the Thermal Label Maker?

The Thermal Label Maker by WorldOfTools is a free, browser-based label design tool that lets you create professional shipping labels, barcode stickers, QR code labels, and price tags for any thermal printer. Whether you're a small business owner shipping orders on Meesho, Amazon FBA, Etsy, or eBay — or a retailer needing price tags — this tool creates print-ready labels instantly with no signup.

Download your label as a PDF (print-ready) or PNG (for importing into printer software like Zebra Designer or DYMO Label). Works on all operating systems in any modern browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which thermal printers does this work with?

This tool generates standard PDF and PNG files that print on any thermal printer — including Zebra ZD220, ZD420, ZP450, DYMO LabelWriter 450/550, Rollo, Munbyn, Brother QL series, and any generic 58mm or 80mm thermal roll printer.

Can I use this for Meesho, Amazon FBA, or Shiprocket shipping?

Yes. Use the 4×6 inch template and fill in the order details. Download as PDF and print on your thermal printer. For platform-specific labels, always verify tracking numbers are accurate before shipping.

What barcode formats are supported?

The tool supports CODE128, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, CODE39, and ITF barcodes. These cover most retail, logistics, and inventory use cases.

Can I generate QR codes for WhatsApp, UPI, or website links?

Yes. In the QR Code tab, enter any text, URL, WhatsApp link, UPI payment string, or contact info (vCard format). The QR code embeds that data directly into your label.

Is my label data stored or uploaded?

No. All label generation, barcode creation, and QR code encoding happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
