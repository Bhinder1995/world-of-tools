→ Build dedicated sections + FAQ for each platform preset. You win this traffic easily.
🟢 VEED watermarks free users but ranks #1

VEED dominates "video compressor online" but adds a watermark on free tier. Users searching "video compressor no watermark free" are actively trying to escape VEED. You are the answer — but your page doesn't shout "no watermark" loudly enough in the opening content.

→ Add "No Watermark, Ever — Even Free" to H1 or H2. Create comparison content vs VEED.
🟢 FreeConvert & Clideo cap at 500MB–1GB free

FreeConvert (1 GB) and Clideo (500 MB) both gate large file compression behind paid plans. The keyword "compress large video file online free" has nobody ranking with a truly free 2 GB tool.

→ Create a dedicated H2: "Compress Large Video Files Up to 2 GB — 100% Free" and target "compress large video file" explicitly.
🟢 No competitor targets "trim and compress" together

You uniquely offer Trim + Compress in one step. None of the pure compressor tools (EzGIF, YouCompress, VideoCompress) have this. "Trim video and compress online free" is completely uncontested.

→ Add "Trim and Compress Video Online Free" as a standalone H2 and FAQ entry targeting this exact phrase.
🔴 Rotato ranks for "browser video compressor" — you don't

Rotato.app (tools.rotato.app/compress) is ranking for browser-side compression terms because they lead with "nothing uploaded to our servers" in their H1. Your page has this message but it's in small print, not the headline.

→ Move "no upload, 100% browser" into the first 50 words of page content. Make it the sub-headline.
🔴 Competitors use "compress video to X MB" — you don't

High-intent searches like "compress video to 16mb", "compress video to 10mb for discord", "compress video to 8mb" are targeted by VideoCompress.ai and FreeConvert via dedicated FAQs. Your page has no target file-size content.

→ Add FAQ items: "How to compress video to 16 MB for WhatsApp?" and "How to compress video to 10 MB for Discord?"
🔴 No dedicated sub-pages for each platform

Clideo (clideo.com/compress-video-for-whatsapp), UniConverter, and Media.io all have dedicated landing pages per platform (WhatsApp, Instagram, Discord, Email). These rank for platform-specific keywords separately from their main page.

→ Create: /compress-video-for-whatsapp, /compress-video-for-instagram, /compress-video-for-youtube as dedicated pages.
🟢 "Video compressor no login" — growing intent

VEED requires login for advanced features, creating user frustration. Searches for "video compressor no login no signup" are growing. Clideo also requires account for no-watermark downloads. You have zero friction — but this isn't mentioned prominently.

→ Add "No Login. No Signup. No Watermark." as a visible trust line near the tool UI and in FAQ.
