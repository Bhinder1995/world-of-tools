Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I put the schema markup code?

You should paste the generated JSON-LD code into the <head> section of your HTML. However, search engines also recognize it if placed in the <body> .

How long does it take for rich snippets to appear?

It depends on how often Google crawls your site. Usually, it takes anywhere from a few days to two weeks for updated rich results to show in SERPs after you add the code.

Does schema markup guarantee a higher ranking?

Schema markup is not a direct ranking factor in the same way backlinks are, but it significantly improves how your site is indexed and presented. Indirectly, higher CTR from rich snippets can signal to Google that your site is high-quality, leading to better rankings over time.
