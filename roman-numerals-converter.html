Frequently Asked Questions

What are Roman numerals?

Roman numerals are a numeral system from ancient Rome using letters: I(1), V(5), X(10), L(50), C(100), D(500), M(1000).

What is 2024 in Roman numerals?

2024 = MMXXIV. Enter 2024 above to verify.

What is the largest number supported?

Standard Roman numerals support 1 to 3999. Numbers beyond 3999 use bar notation which is not widely standardized.

What is IX in numbers?

IX = 9. When a smaller numeral precedes a larger one it is subtracted: I before X = 10 - 1 = 9.
