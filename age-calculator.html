The Math: Why Manual Calculation Fails

Subtracting your birth year from the current year is often inaccurate because it ignores the months and days elapsed. For exact forms, you need the "Borrowing" method.

💡 The Leap Year Factor

Our algorithm handles the "February 29th" edge case perfectly. If you were born on a leap day, the calculator accurately determines your age during non-leap years, ensuring you never miss a day of your life's tally.
