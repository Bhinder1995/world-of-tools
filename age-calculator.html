Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate age from date of birth?

Subtract your birth date from today's date. Our calculator handles leap years and varying month lengths automatically for exact precision.

How old am I if I was born in 1990?

If born on January 1, 1990, you would be 35 years old as of January 1, 2025. Use our calculator with your exact DOB for a precise answer.

How to calculate age difference between two people?

Enter the first person's DOB, note the result, then enter the second person's DOB. The difference gives the age gap.

Why is age calculation important for government forms?

Many Indian government forms, job applications, school admissions, and insurance documents require exact age in years and months, making a precise calculator essential.

Can I calculate someone's age on a past or future date?

Yes, use the 'Calculate age on a specific date' option to find someone's age at any point in time — useful for legal documents, retroactive age verification, etc.
