How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Select your Date of Birth using the calendar picker.
  2. Click the "Calculate Age" button.
  3. View your exact age displayed in years, months, and days.
  4. See additional details like total days, hours, and minutes lived!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How accurate is this Age Calculator?

This calculator is 100% accurate. It calculates the exact duration between your birth date and the current date, taking into account leap years and the varying number of days in each month.

Does this calculator store my birth date?

No, we do not store any of your personal information. All calculations are performed instantly in your browser (client-side), ensuring your privacy.

Can I calculate age for a future date?

Currently, this tool determines your age based on "today." However, you can input a past date as your "Date of Birth" to find the duration between that date and today.
