How to Calculate Age for Legal Documents in India

In India, many official forms (like passport applications, school admissions, and competitive exams) require you to state your exact age as of a specific reference date. For example, an exam might require you to be "between 18 and 25 years old as of July 1st, 2026."

Our tool's "Specific Date" tab is perfect for this. Simply enter your Date of Birth and the required reference date to get an accurate calculation that adheres to standard Indian administrative rules. This eliminates manual errors and ensures your applications are technically correct.

Frequently Asked Questions

How old am I if I was born in 1990?

If born in 1990, you are 35 years old in 2025 (or 34 if your birthday hasn't occurred yet this year). Use the calculator for your exact age with months and days.

How do I calculate age from date of birth?

Subtract your birth year from the current year, then adjust by -1 if your birthday hasn't passed yet this year. For exact months and days, use this age calculator.

How many days old am I?

Multiply your completed years by 365, add days for each leap year, then add remaining months and days. Or just use this tool — it computes it instantly.

Can I calculate age between two specific dates?

Yes. Enter any start date (as DOB) and any end date (as target date) to find the exact duration between two dates.

Does the calculator handle leap year birthdays (Feb 29)?

Yes. If you were born on February 29, the calculator accurately handles your birthday in non-leap years.

What if I only know my birth year, not the exact date?

You can enter January 1 of your birth year for an approximate calculation.

Is my date of birth stored or shared?

No. All calculations run in your browser. Your DOB is never uploaded or stored anywhere.

Can I use this for age verification purposes?

Yes, it gives a precise age for filling forms, age verification, legal documents, and school admissions.
