How Age is Calculated

📅 Calculation Methodology

Age is calculated by comparing your Date of Birth (DOB) with a reference date (today's date or a specific future/past date). The system first calculates full years, then remaining months, and finally the precise number of days.

🌎 Leap Year Explanation

Leap years occur every 4 years to keep our calendar in alignment with the Earth's orbit. Our tool automatically accounts for February 29th. If you were born on a leap day, your "birthday" in non-leap years is typically celebrated on February 28th or March 1st—our calculator handles these transitions with mathematical precision.

🧠 Chronological vs. Biological Age

Chronological Age is the amount of time that has passed from your birth to the given date (what this tool calculates). Biological Age refers to how old your cells and tissues seem based on physiological biomarkers, lifestyle, and genetics. While you can't change your chronological age, healthy habits can "lower" your biological age!

🌗 Why Months and Days Differ?

Because months have varying lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days), calculating the "exact" age in months is a convention. Our tool uses the Calendar Month method, which is the standard used for legal and official purposes worldwide.
