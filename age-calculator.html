What is the Age Calculator?

The Age Calculator by WorldOfTools is a free, instant, and privacy-first utility to calculate your exact age from any date of birth. Whether you're filling a government form, verifying age for Aadhar or PAN, calculating age for school admission eligibility, or simply curious about how many days you've been alive — this tool gives you precise results in seconds.

Unlike other online tools, all processing happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your date of birth is never sent to any server, never stored, and never tracked.

How to Calculate Age for Legal Documents in India

In India, many official forms (passport, Aadhar, school admissions, UPSC, SSC, competitive exams) require you to state your exact age as of a specific reference date. For example, an exam might require you to be "between 18 and 25 years old as of July 1st, 2026."

Use the Specific Date tab — enter your Date of Birth and the required reference date to get a calculation that adheres to standard Indian administrative rules. Eliminate manual errors from your applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

How old am I if I was born in 1990?

If born in 1990, you are 36 years old in 2026 (or 35 if your birthday hasn't occurred yet this year). Use this calculator for your exact age with months and days.

How do I calculate age from date of birth?

Subtract your birth year from the current year, then adjust by -1 if your birthday hasn't passed yet this year. For exact months and days, just use this tool — it computes it instantly in your browser.

How many days old am I?

Multiply your completed years by 365, add days for each leap year, then add remaining months and days. Or just enter your DOB above — the tool computes it instantly.

Can I calculate age between two specific dates?

Yes. Use the "Specific Date" tab — enter any start date (as DOB) and any end date to find the exact duration between two dates. Perfect for contract durations, visa validity, and exam eligibility.

Does the calculator handle leap year birthdays (Feb 29)?

Yes. If you were born on February 29, the calculator accurately handles your birthday in non-leap years, using the closest valid date.

Is my date of birth stored or shared?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your DOB is never uploaded to any server, stored in any database, or shared with anyone.

Can I use this for age verification / legal purposes?

Yes. This tool gives a precise age for filling forms, age verification, legal documents, Aadhar registration, school admissions, and competitive exam eligibility checks.
