Safely encode special characters for URLs or decode them back to plain text.

What is URL Encoding?

URL encoding (Percentage encoding) converts characters that have special meaning in a URL into a format that can be safely transmitted over the internet. For example, a space becomes %20.

Why use this tool?

  • Fix Broken Links: Ensure your query parameters are correctly formatted.
  • Developer Friendly: Quickly decode API endpoints or tracking URLs to read their parameters.
  • Total Privacy: Just like our other tools, processing is 100% client-side. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What characters are encoded?

Characters like { space }, &, =, ?, #, and non-ASCII characters are converted into their hex equivalents preceded by a percent sign.

Does it support UTF-8?

Yes, this tool correctly handles UTF-8 characters, ensuring special symbols and international characters are properly encoded.
