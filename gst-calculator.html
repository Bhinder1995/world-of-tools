About Our Online GST Calculator

Our **GST Calculator** is designed to simplify the complex tax calculations used in India and several other countries. Whether you're a business owner, a consumer, or a freelancer, understanding how Goods and Services Tax affects your pricing is crucial for financial transparency.

GST Inclusive vs Exclusive: What's the Difference?

  • GST Exclusive: This is when you want to add tax to a base price. It's commonly used when quoting services to clients.
  • GST Inclusive: This is when the price already includes the tax. Use this to find out how much of your receipt total was actually tax.

Standard GST Slabs in India

Our tool supports all official Indian tax slabs:

  • 3%: For gold and silver ornaments.
  • 5%: For essential household items and packaged food.
  • 12%: For processed foods and apparel.
  • 18%: The standard rate for services and electronic goods.
  • 28%: For luxury items and luxury cars.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this calculator compliant with CGST and SGST?

Yes. In standard domestic transactions, the 'GST Amount' calculated by our tool is simply split evenly between CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST).

Can I enter a custom tax rate?

Absolutely. Use the 'Custom %' field to enter any tax rate from 0% to 100% for specific products or international tax regions.
