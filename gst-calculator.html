How It Works

Adding GST:

- GST Amount = Base Price × (GST Rate / 100)
- Final Price = Base Price + GST Amount
- Example: ₹1000 base + 18% GST = ₹180 GST = ₹1180 total

Removing GST (Reverse GST):

- Base Price = Inclusive Price / (1 + GST Rate / 100)
- GST Amount = Inclusive Price − Base Price
- Example: ₹1180 inclusive ÷ 1.18 = ₹1000 base + ₹180 GST

CGST / SGST Split (Intra-State):

- CGST = GST Amount / 2
- SGST = GST Amount / 2
- Example: ₹180 GST = ₹90 CGST + ₹90 SGST

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I calculate 18% GST on ₹5000?

GST Amount = 5000 × 18 / 100 = ₹900. Final price = 5000 + 900 = **₹5,900**.

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Base Price = Inclusive Price / (1 + GST%/100). Example: ₹5900 / 1.18 = **₹5,000**.

What are the GST slabs in India?

India has four main GST slabs: **5%** (essential goods), **12%** (standard goods), **18%** (most services and manufactured goods), and **28%** (luxury goods). A 0% (nil) slab also exists for exempt goods.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

For intra-state sales: CGST + SGST = Total GST (each half). For inter-state sales: IGST = Full GST amount.

Can I use this for B2B invoices?

Yes. Use it for any business invoice — retail, wholesale, freelance, or B2B. It gives you all the tax component details needed for GST invoicing.

Does the GST calculator store my data?

No. All calculations run in your browser and are never stored, logged, or shared.

Is there GST on services?

Yes. Most services attract 18% GST in India. Some are at 5% or 12%. Check the GST rate schedule for your specific service category.

Can I calculate GST for a composition scheme?

The composition scheme has different rates (1%, 2%, 5% of turnover). Enter your applicable rate as a custom percentage in this calculator.
