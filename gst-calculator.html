How the GST Toolkit Works

🏢 Simple Mode vs. Invoice Mode

Use Simple Mode for quick one-off calculations (adding or removing GST). Switch to Invoice Mode when you have multiple products with different GST rates. It acts as a mini billing tool, calculating totals and tax splits for your entire list.

⚖️ CGST, SGST, and IGST Split

Our toolkit automatically handles tax splits. For Intra-State transactions, it splits GST into CGST (Central) and SGST (State) equally (50/50). For Inter-State transactions, it applies the full amount as IGST (Integrated GST), which is critical for accurate B2B invoicing.

📊 Visual Data & Smart Insights

The Pie Chart provides a quick visual of your tax-to-base ratio. Our Smart Insights engine alerts you if a high tax component (e.g., 28% luxury slab) is detected, helping you verify if the correct GST slab is being applied.

Key Features for Professionals:

  • Multi-Item Table: Add as many products as you need with individual quantities and rates.
  • Instant Export: Download your calculation summary as a PDF Invoice or CSV Data file.
  • Reverse GST: Easily find the original base price from a GST-inclusive total.
  • 100% Client-Side: No data is ever sent to a server; your privacy is our priority.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I calculate GST for different items in one go?

Yes! Use the Invoice Mode to add multiple rows. You can set a different GST rate (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) for each product, and the tool will calculate the total taxable value and tax split.

When should I select Intra-State vs. Inter-State?

Select Intra-State if the buyer and seller are in the same state (CGST + SGST applies). Select Inter-State if they are in different states (IGST applies).

How do I download my GST calculation?

Once you have your results, scroll down to the "Exports" section. You can click PDF Invoice for a formatted summary or CSV Export for data processing in Excel.

Is my data safe in Invoice Mode?

Absolutely. We do not store any item names, prices, or quantities. All invoice generation happens locally in your browser memory and is cleared when you refresh the page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 18% GST on an amount?

GST = Base x 18/100. Rs.5000 x 18% = Rs.900 GST. Final = Rs.5900.

How do I remove GST from a total?

Divide inclusive price by (1 + GST%/100). Rs.5900 / 1.18 = Rs.5000 base.

What are the GST slabs in India 2026?

Four slabs: 5% (essentials), 12% (standard goods), 18% (services), 28% (luxury).

What is CGST vs SGST?

Intra-state: CGST + SGST = Total GST (each half). Inter-state: IGST = full amount.

Is my data stored?

No. All calculations run in your browser only. Nothing is uploaded.
