Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is GST Exclusive?

GST Exclusive means the tax is calculated on top of the product price. For example, if a product costs ₹100 and GST is 18%, the total you pay is ₹118.

What is GST Inclusive?

GST Inclusive means the tax is already part of the total price. For example, if you pay ₹118 for a product with 18% GST (Inclusive), the actual product price is ₹100 and tax is ₹18.

Which GST rates are supported?

We support all standard Indian GST slabs: 3% (Gold), 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. You can also enter any custom percentage.
