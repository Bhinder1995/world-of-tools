Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is GST Inclusive and Exclusive?

GST Exclusive means the price does not include tax yet. GST Inclusive means the price already includes the tax amount.

Is this the best GST calculator for India?

Yes! It supports all Indian standard GST slabs (3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and provides a clean breakdown for businesses and consumers.

How do I calculate GST?

To add GST, multiply the amount by (GST rate/100). To remove GST, divide the amount by (1 + GST rate/100).

Can I use a custom GST rate?

Yes! Our calculator supports any custom GST percentage. Just enter your rate in the custom field and the breakdown updates immediately.
