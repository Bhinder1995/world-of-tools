What is the GST Calculator?

The GST Calculator by WorldOfTools is a free, instant, browser-based tool to calculate Goods and Services Tax for any price in India. Whether you're a business owner creating invoices, a freelancer computing service tax, or a consumer checking if a price is GST-inclusive — this tool gives you precise results instantly.

Supports all standard Indian GST slabs: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%, plus any custom percentage. The IGST toggle automatically switches between intra-state (CGST+SGST) and inter-state (IGST) calculation modes.

All processing happens 100% in your browser. No data is ever sent to any server.

How to Calculate GST — Step by Step

1

Choose Mode — "Add GST" to calculate the final invoice price. "Remove GST" to find the base cost from an inclusive price. "Bulk Items" for full invoice totals.

2

Enter Amount — Type the price in rupees. Use decimals for paise accuracy (e.g., 1450.50).

3

Select GST Slab — Choose from 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% or enter a custom rate. Toggle IGST for interstate transactions.

4

Click Calculate — Instantly get the total amount, CGST, SGST (or IGST), and base price with a full tax breakdown table.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add GST to a price?

Multiply the base price by (1 + GST%/100). For ₹1,000 at 18% GST: ₹1,000 × 1.18 = ₹1,180. The Add GST tab above does this instantly.

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Divide the inclusive price by (1 + GST%/100). For ₹1,180 at 18%: ₹1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000 base. Use the Remove GST tab above.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

CGST (Central GST) + SGST (State GST) apply to intra-state transactions — each is half the total GST rate. IGST (Integrated GST) applies to inter-state transactions and equals the full GST rate. The total tax burden is the same.

What is the GST rate on software services?

Software services (SaaS, IT services, consulting) generally attract 18% GST. Custom software development and IT-enabled services are also typically taxed at 18%.

Can I calculate GST for multiple items at once?

Yes! Use the Bulk Items tab above. Add each item with its price and GST slab, and the calculator will compute the total GST for your complete invoice.
