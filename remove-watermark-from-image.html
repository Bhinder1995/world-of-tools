⚠️ Limitations

This tool uses canvas-based inpainting — it works best for watermarks on relatively uniform or textured backgrounds. For complex scene backgrounds (e.g. watermark over a face), the result may need manual touch-up. For perfect removal on complex images, dedicated desktop tools like GIMP or Photoshop may give better results.

📚 About This Tool

This free watermark remover uses the HTML5 Canvas API with a content-aware inpainting algorithm. When you paint over a watermark and click Remove, the tool samples pixel data from the surrounding regions and intelligently reconstructs the background — similar to how Photoshop's "Content-Aware Fill" works, but running entirely in your browser.

Unlike server-based tools that upload your images to cloud servers (raising privacy concerns), this tool processes everything locally. Your photos stay on your device. This makes it safe for sensitive documents, business images, or personal photos where privacy matters.
