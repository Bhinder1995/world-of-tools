What Is a Typing Speed Test?

A typing speed test measures how many words you type per minute (WPM) and your accuracy percentage. It helps you track improvement over time and benchmark your performance against average typists. This is useful for students, job seekers, data entry professionals, and anyone wanting to type faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good typing speed in WPM?

Average typist: 40–50 WPM. Professional typist: 65–75 WPM. Excellent: 80–100+ WPM.

What does WPM mean?

WPM = Words Per Minute. One "word" is standardized as 5 keystrokes including spaces.

How can I improve my typing speed?

Practice daily with touch typing technique. Focus on accuracy first, then speed. Aim for 15–30 minutes of practice per day.

What is net WPM?

Net WPM = Gross WPM minus errors per minute. It accounts for mistakes and gives a more accurate measure.

Does typing speed matter for a job?

Data entry roles require 60+ WPM. Customer service and secretarial jobs need 50+ WPM. Office workers benefit from 40+ WPM.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it works on mobile with a hardware or on-screen keyboard, though a physical keyboard gives the most accurate results.
