What is a Strong Password Generator?

The Password Generator by WorldOfTools creates cryptographically secure, random passwords using your browser's built-in window.crypto.getRandomValues() API — the same standard used by banks and security software. Your password is generated entirely on your device and never transmitted to any server.

Unlike weak passwords like "password123" or "abc@2024", a strong random password uses a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols to create billions of combinations that can't be guessed or brute-forced in any reasonable timeframe.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should a secure password be?

At least 16 characters for regular accounts. For banking or email — use 20+ characters. The longer the password, the exponentially harder it is to brute-force.

Is it safe to use an online password generator?

Yes — as long as it's 100% browser-based like ours. We use window.crypto.getRandomValues() and your password is never sent to any server.

What makes a password strong?

Length (16+ characters), a mix of uppercase/lowercase/numbers/symbols, no dictionary words, and no personal information. Avoid patterns like "123456" or "qwerty".

How often should I change my passwords?

Change immediately after a data breach. For critical accounts (banking, email), rotate every 3–6 months. Use unique passwords for every service.

What is password entropy?

Entropy measures the unpredictability of a password in bits. A 128-bit entropy password would take longer than the age of the universe to crack using even the most powerful computers.

Should I use a password manager?

Yes. Tools like Bitwarden (free, open source), 1Password, or Dashlane let you store unique strong passwords for every account without having to remember them all.
