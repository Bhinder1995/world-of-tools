Secure Password Generation: How It Works

Our tool generates high-entropy passwords entirely in your browser using secure cryptographic functions.

1

Cryptographic Randomness

Our tool uses the window.crypto.getRandomValues() API. Unlike standard Math.random(), this provides a cryptographically strong source of entropy, ensuring passwords cannot be predicted based on previous outputs.

2

Local Computation

Every password is generated directly in your browser. No data is ever sent to our servers, and no person (including us) ever sees your generated credentials. This is the ultimate "Zero Trust" approach to security.

3

Entropy-Based Scoring

We calculate the complexity of your password using information theory. High entropy means there are billions of possible combinations, making it mathematically impossible for any modern computer to brute-force in a reasonable timeframe.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why should I use a generated password?

Most humans tend to use predictable patterns (like names, birthdays, or keyboard patterns). Hackers use dictionaries and massive databases of common patterns to crack these in milliseconds. A random generator removes all patterns, making the password truly unique.

What is a "Passphrase" and why is it good?

A passphrase is a sequence of random words. They are often easier to remember than random strings of characters while remaining just as secure (or even more so) due to their length. A 4-word passphrase can be harder to crack than a 12-character random string.

Does this tool store my history online?

No. We use your browser's localStorage, which is a private storage area on your device. Only your browser can access this, and it is never uploaded to the internet. You can clear it at any time using the "Clear All" button.
