The Power of Systematic Planning

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is more than just a savings tool; it's a wealth-building strategy that leverages compounding and rupee-cost averaging to mitigate market volatility.

📉 Rupee Cost Averaging

Buy more units when prices are low and fewer when high, effectively averaging out your purchase cost without needing to "time the market."

⏳ Power of Compounding

Your returns earn their own returns. Over 10-20 years, the wealth gain often far exceeds the total capital invested.

SIP vs. Lumpsum: Which is Better?

While a Lumpsum investment puts your full capital to work from Day 1, an SIP is ideal for regular earners. SIPs offer disciplined saving and protect against sudden market crashes by spreading entry points across different market cycles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good SIP return rate?

Historically, Indian equity mutual funds have delivered 12–15% CAGR over long periods (10+ years). Debt funds typically offer 6–8%.

Can I stop a SIP anytime?

Yes. Most mutual funds allow you to pause, stop, or redeem your SIP units at any time without penalties, providing excellent liquidity.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All calculations are performed locally in your browser. We never store or transmit your investment amounts or financial goals.
