Frequently Asked Questions

How many cm in an inch?

1 inch equals exactly 2.54 centimetres. Conversely, 1 cm = 0.393701 inches. Use the Length tab above for any value.

How do I convert kg to lbs?

Multiply kilograms by 2.20462 to get pounds. For example, 70 kg = 154.32 lbs. Use the Weight tab for instant conversion.

Celsius to Fahrenheit formula?

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. For Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. For example, 37°C = 98.6°F (body temperature).

Is the converter accurate?

Yes. All conversion factors follow internationally accepted SI standard values, accurate to 10+ decimal places. Temperature uses exact mathematical formulas, not approximations.

Can I convert between metric and imperial?

Yes. All categories support both metric and imperial units. For length, for example, you can convert between mm, cm, m, km, inches, feet, yards, and miles freely.
