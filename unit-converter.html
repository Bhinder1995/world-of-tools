Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert cm to inches?

Divide centimeters by 2.54. Example: 30 cm ÷ 2.54 = 11.81 inches. Use this tool for instant conversion.

How do I convert kg to lbs?

Multiply kg by 2.20462. Example: 70 kg × 2.20462 = 154.32 lbs.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Formula: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Example: 25°C = (25 × 1.8) + 32 = 77°F.

What is 1 meter in feet?

1 meter = 3.28084 feet.

How do I convert liters to gallons?

1 liter = 0.264172 US gallons. Divide liters by 3.78541 for gallons.

Does this work offline?

Yes — once the page loads, all conversion logic runs locally and works without internet.

Is the converter accurate?

Yes. All conversion factors are based on internationally accepted NIST and ISO standards.
