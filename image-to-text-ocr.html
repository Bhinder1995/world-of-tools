What is the Image to Text OCR?

The Image to Text OCR (Optical Character Recognition) tool by WorldOfTools is a high-performance utility that converts printed or handwritten text within images into machine-encoded text. Whether you have a scanned document, a photo of a receipt, or a screenshot of a webpage, our tool extracts the text instantly, allowing you to edit, copy, and share it without manual retyping.

How to Use OCR — Step-by-Step

1

Upload Image: Select your JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo from your device.

2

Select Language: Choose the primary language of the text in the image (e.g., Hindi, English).

3

Copy & Save: Review the extracted text, copy it to your clipboard, or download it as a .txt file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract text from a blurry photo?

While high resolution is best, our AI engine can often recover text from slightly blurry images. For best results, ensure the text is well-lit and flat.

Photo se text kaise copy karein? (Hindi)

Apni photo upload karein, 'Copy Text' button par click karein, aur kahin bhi paste karein. Yeh tool poori tarah surakshit (private) hai.

Is there a file size limit?

Yes, we support images up to 15MB to ensure smooth local processing in your browser.
