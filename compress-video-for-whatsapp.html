Why Compress Video for WhatsApp?

WhatsApp has a strict 16 MB file size limit for videos sent as media. While you can send larger files as 'Documents,' they don't show a player in the chat. Our WhatsApp video compressor ensures your video is small enough to play directly in the chat, without losing the quality visible on a smartphone screen. Best of all, it's 100% free and has no watermark.
