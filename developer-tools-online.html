No. WorldOfTools is a zero-knowledge platform. All formatting, decoding, and generation happen locally in your browser's memory. We don't have a database for your inputs.

Yes! Our JSON tool allows you to both beautify for readability and minify for production use with a single toggle.

Absolutely. Unlike many online JWT tools that send your tokens to a server, ours decodes them entirely in your browser using local JavaScript. Your secrets remain on your machine.
