What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text used by designers, developers, and typesetters since the 1500s to fill space in mockups and layouts. It originates from Cicero's philosophical treatise De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum (On the Ends of Good and Evil), written around 45 BC. The standard passage beginning with "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet..." is a scrambled Latin excerpt that has been used in printing and typesetting as a standard "dummy text" for over five centuries.

The reason designers use Lorem Ipsum rather than readable text is that it allows viewers to focus on visual elements like layout, typography, and spacing — without being distracted by the actual content of the text.
