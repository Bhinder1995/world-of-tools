Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does compressing an image lower its quality?

Yes, to reduce file size, some image data is discarded. However, at 80-90% quality, the difference is often invisible to the human eye while the file size can be reduced by up to 70%.

What is the best quality setting for websites?

For most websites, a setting between 70% and 85% is the sweet spot for fast loading times and crisp visuals.

Can I compress PNG files?

Yes! If you upload a PNG, our tool will compress it using its internal optimization path, though JPG often sees higher compression ratios for complex photos.

Is it safe to compress private photos here?

Absolutely. Our tool is 100% client-side. Your images are never uploaded to any server and remain entirely within your browser at all times.
