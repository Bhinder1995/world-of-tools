Frequently Asked Questions

How to compress an image without losing quality?

Use a quality level of 75–85% for JPEG — this removes imperceptible data while keeping visual quality near-identical. PNG compression is lossless.

What is the ideal image size for a website?

Web images should generally be under 200KB. Hero images can be up to 500KB. Use WEBP format for 25–35% better compression than JPG/PNG at equal quality.

How to reduce image size for WhatsApp without quality loss?

Compress the image to about 200–500KB at 80% quality. WhatsApp accepts images up to 16MB but recommends smaller sizes for faster sharing.

Is my image safe when I compress it here?

Yes — your image is processed entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. It is never uploaded to any server. Your files stay private.

How to resize an image to passport size online?

Set dimensions to 35×45mm (standard Indian passport) or 2×2 inches (US passport) in the resize fields and download.
