Frequently Asked Questions

What is sin, cos, tan in a scientific calculator?

Trigonometric functions: sin = opposite/hypotenuse, cos = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan = opposite/adjacent. Used in geometry, physics, and engineering.

How do I calculate log base 10?

Press `log` and enter your number. Example: log(1000) = 3 (because 10³ = 1000).

What is the difference between `ln` and `log`?

`log` is logarithm base 10. `ln` is the natural logarithm (base e ≈ 2.71828). Both are available on this calculator.

How do I calculate factorial (n!)?

Enter the number and press `n!`. Example: 5! = 5×4×3×2×1 = 120.

Can I use this for physics problems?

Yes — it handles the calculations needed for kinematics, vectors, wave equations, and other physics formulas requiring trigonometry and exponents.
