Video compression works by reducing bitrate — the amount of data used per second of video. The High Quality preset reduces file size by ~25% while preserving near-original clarity, ideal for YouTube or professional use. The Small File preset can shrink a video by 70–80%, perfect for WhatsApp or messaging apps.

Combining resolution reduction with medium quality compression is the fastest way to make a video small enough for email or social media.

📚 Educational Content — Understanding Video Compression

Video compression is the process of reducing a video file's data size by removing redundant or less important visual information. Modern codecs like H.264 (AVC) and H.265 (HEVC) achieve this through techniques like inter-frame prediction (comparing consecutive frames and only storing the differences), spatial compression within individual frames, and quantization of frequency coefficients. The result is a much smaller file that looks virtually identical to the human eye.

The key tradeoff is between bitrate (data per second) and visual quality . A higher bitrate means more detail is preserved, while a lower bitrate produces a smaller file at the cost of some sharpness, especially in fast-moving scenes. For most everyday sharing purposes — WhatsApp messages, Instagram posts, email attachments — a medium quality compression that cuts file size by 40–60% is virtually indistinguishable from the original when viewed on a phone screen.
