Image Format Guide — Which Format Should You Use?

📷 JPEG

Best for photos. Lossy compression. No transparency. Universal compatibility.

🖼️ PNG

Best for graphics, logos, screenshots. Lossless. Supports transparency.

🌐 WebP

Best for web. Smallest file at similar quality. Supports transparency. Modern browsers.

🎞️ GIF

For simple animations. Limited to 256 colors. Best for basic moving graphics.

🗺️ BMP

Uncompressed bitmap. Largest files. Compatible with old Windows software.

✨ AVIF

Next-gen format. Even smaller than WebP. Excellent quality. Growing browser support.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert PNG to JPG online?

Upload your PNG, select JPEG as the output format, adjust quality (90% is ideal), and click Convert. The JPG downloads instantly. No signup needed.

Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?

No. Converting a lossy JPG to PNG doesn't restore lost quality — it only prevents further quality loss during future saves.

What's the best format for website images?

WebP offers the best balance of quality and file size for modern websites. Use JPEG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency, WebP as a universal modern format.

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes. Use the Batch Convert tab to upload and convert up to 20 images simultaneously.

Does this tool keep images private?

Yes. All conversion happens in your browser. Images are never uploaded to any server.
