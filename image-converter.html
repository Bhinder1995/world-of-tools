Why Convert in the Browser?

Traditional online converters require you to upload your files to their servers. This poses a privacy risk and depends on your internet upload speed. Our tool uses the native Browser APIs and WebAssembly to process images on your device. It's faster, safer, and works even with large files.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I convert images without uploading them?

Our tool runs entirely on your device. When you select a file, the browser reads it into memory and uses the Canvas API to re-encode it into your chosen format. No data is sent to our servers.

Does it support HEIC (iPhone) photos?

Yes! We use a special library called heic2any to handle HEIC files, allowing you to convert them to standard formats like JPG or PNG directly in your browser.

Is there a file size limit?

We recommend processing files under 20MB for the best experience, although larger files may work depending on your computer's RAM. We do not impose any artificial limits.
