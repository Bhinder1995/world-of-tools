Frequently Asked Questions

What is number to words used for?

Writing cheques, filling legal documents, invoices, bank forms, and government applications where numeric values must be written in words.

How to write 1 lakh in words?

1,00,000 = One Lakh. In Indian Rupees format: One Lakh Rupees Only.

How to write 10 crore in words?

10,00,00,000 = Ten Crore. In Rupees: Ten Crore Rupees Only.
