Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which hash is most secure?

SHA-256 and SHA-512 are currently considered secure for general use. MD5 and SHA-1 are considered cryptographically broken and should only be used for checksums or legacy compatibility.

Can I reverse a hash?

Cryptographic hashes are one-way functions. You cannot "decrypt" a hash back into text. However, short hashes can sometimes be found in "rainbow tables" containing pre-computed hashes.
