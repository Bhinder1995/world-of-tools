Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I calculate a 20% discount?

Use the "Find Percentage" tool. Enter 20 in the percentage box and your total price in the number box. The result is your discount amount.

What is the percentage increase formula?

Percentage Increase = [(New Value - Old Value) / Old Value] x 100. Our "Percentage Change" tool handles this automatically for you.
