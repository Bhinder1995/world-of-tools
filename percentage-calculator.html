Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I calculate a 20% discount?

Use the "Find Percentage" tool. Enter 20 in the percentage box and your total price in the number box. The result is your discount amount.

What is the percentage increase formula?

Percentage Increase = [(New Value - Old Value) / Old Value] x 100. Our "Percentage Change" tool handles this automatically for you.

Is this calculator free for students?

Yes! WorldOfTools is designed to be a free educational and professional resource for everyone.

Can I calculate profit margin with this tool?

Yes! Use the "Find Part (%)" section: enter your profit in the first box and revenue in the second to get your profit margin percentage instantly.
