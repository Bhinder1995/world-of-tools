How to Use Percentage Calculator

Our percentage calculator offers three different modes to solve common math problems:

  1. Find Percentage: Use this to find a portion of a number. (e.g., "What is 20% of 150?")
  2. Find Part: Use this to determine what percentage one number is of another. (e.g., "25 is what % of 100?")
  3. Percentage Change: Use this to calculate the increase or decrease between two values. (e.g., "From 50 to 80")

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I calculate a discount?

To calculate a discount, use the "Find Percentage" tool. Enter the discount percentage (e.g., 20) and the original price (e.g., 100). The result is the amount you save. Subtract that from the original price to get the final price.

What is the formula for percentage change?

The formula is: ((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) × 100. If the result is positive, it's an increase; if negative, it's a decrease.
