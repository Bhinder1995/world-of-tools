Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. Example: 20% of 450 = (20 × 450) / 100 = 90.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Formula: ((New Value − Old Value) / Old Value) × 100. Example: from 200 to 250 = ((250 − 200) / 200) × 100 = 25% increase.

How do I find the original price after a discount?

Use reverse percentage: Original Price = Discounted Price / (1 − Discount% / 100). Example: ₹850 after 15% off → 850 / 0.85 = ₹1000.

How to calculate percentage decrease?

((Old Value − New Value) / Old Value) × 100. Example: from 500 to 400 = ((500 − 400) / 500) × 100 = 20% decrease.

What percentage is 75 of 300?

(75 / 300) × 100 = 25%. Use this tool to instantly compute any such percentage question.
