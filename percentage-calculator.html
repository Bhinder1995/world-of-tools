How It Works

The percentage calculator applies the fundamental percentage formula:

- Basic percentage: Result = (Percentage / 100) × Number

- Percentage increase: ((New Value − Old Value) / Old Value) × 100
- Percentage decrease: ((Old Value − New Value) / Old Value) × 100
- Reverse percentage: Original = Final Price / (1 − Discount% / 100)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. Example: 20% of 450 = (20 × 450) / 100 = 90.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Formula: ((New Value − Old Value) / Old Value) × 100. Example: from 200 to 250 = ((250 − 200) / 200) × 100 = 25% increase.

How do I find the original price after a discount?

Use reverse percentage: Original Price = Discounted Price / (1 − Discount% / 100). Example: ₹850 after 15% off → 850 / 0.85 = ₹1000.

How to calculate percentage decrease?

((Old Value − New Value) / Old Value) × 100. Example: from 500 to 400 = ((500 − 400) / 500) × 100 = 20% decrease.

What percentage is 75 of 300?

(75 / 300) × 100 = 25%. Use this tool to instantly compute any such percentage question.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate percentage of marks?

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100. For example: 450 out of 500 = (450÷500)×100 = 90%.

How to convert CGPA to percentage?

Most Indian universities use the formula: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5 (for CBSE) or CGPA × 10 - 7.5 (for VTU). Check your specific university's conversion formula.

How to calculate a 20% discount?

Discounted Price = Original Price × (1 - Discount%/100). For a ₹500 item at 20% off: ₹500 × 0.80 = ₹400.

How to calculate percentage increase?

% Increase = ((New Value - Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100. Example: price went from ₹100 to ₹120 = 20% increase.
