Frequently Asked Questions

What is a favicon?

A favicon is the small icon shown in browser tabs, bookmarks, and address bars that represents your website. It's typically a 16×16 or 32×32 pixel image in ICO or PNG format.

What image formats can I upload?

PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, GIF, and BMP. For best results use a square image (e.g., your logo) with a transparent background, at least 256×256 px.

What favicon sizes should I generate?

Essential sizes: 16×16 (browser tab), 32×32 (taskbar), 180×180 (Apple Touch), 192×192 (Android). For full coverage generate all sizes — this tool makes it instant.

How do I add a favicon to my website?

Place the favicon files in your website root and paste the generated HTML snippet inside your <head> tag. Our tool generates the exact HTML for you.

ICO vs PNG — which should I use?

Use both for best compatibility. ICO works in all browsers including legacy IE. PNG is supported by all modern browsers and required for Apple Touch Icons. Best practice is to use favicon.ico at root plus PNG link tags in HTML.

Is this favicon generator free?

Yes, 100% free. No signup, no watermark, no file size limit. All processing is done in your browser — your image never leaves your device.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. We never see, store, or share your images.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, fully responsive — works on smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

📚 Educational Content — Deep Dive

A favicon (short for "favourite icon") was introduced by Internet Explorer 5 in 1999 and standardised by the W3C. Despite its tiny size, a favicon plays an important role in your website's branding and user recognition — it's the first visual identifier users see when they have multiple tabs open.

Modern websites need more than just a single favicon.ico. The manifest.json for Progressive Web Apps (PWA) requires 192×192 and 512×512 PNG icons. Apple's Safari and iOS Safari require a special "apple-touch-icon" at 180×180. Chrome and Edge use 32×32 for their tab bar. Generating all sizes from one source image ensures consistency across all platforms.

Our tool uses the HTML5 Canvas API to draw your image at every required size, applying your chosen background, padding, and corner radius before exporting as a PNG data URL — all entirely within your browser. No data ever touches our servers, making this the most private favicon tool available online.
