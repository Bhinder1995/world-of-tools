What is a Favicon and Why Does Your Website Need One?

    
A favicon (short for "favorites icon") is the small icon displayed in browser tabs, bookmarks, history, and address bars. It's the visual identity of your website in every browser interaction. Modern websites need multiple favicon sizes to display correctly across all devices and contexts — from tiny 16×16 browser tabs to 512×512 Android PWA splash screens.

    
Without a proper favicon, your site shows a generic browser icon — making it harder to identify in tabs and bookmarks, reducing brand recognition, and signaling unprofessionalism to visitors.

Frequently Asked Questions

    
What size should a favicon be?
Modern websites need 16×16, 32×32, and 48×48 for browser tabs, 180×180 for Apple devices, and 192×192 and 512×512 for Android PWA. This generator creates all sizes in one click.

    
How do I add a favicon to my website?
Download the favicon files, upload them to your website root, then paste the HTML code from this generator into your page's <head> section. The code is pre-written with all necessary link tags.

    
Can I create a favicon from text or emoji?
Yes! Use the Text/Emoji tab. Enter a letter, initials, or any emoji, choose colors and shape, and generate professional favicons instantly.

    
What image format is best for favicons?
Use PNG for modern browsers (best quality). ICO is the legacy format still used for broad compatibility. This tool generates both. For best results, use a square image as the source.
