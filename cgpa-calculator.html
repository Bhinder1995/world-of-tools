❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is CGPA calculated in India?

CGPA = Σ(Grade Points × Credits) ÷ Σ(Credits). For example, if you scored 9 in a 4-credit subject and 8 in a 3-credit subject: CGPA = (9×4 + 8×3) / (4+3) = (36+24)/7 = 60/7 ≈ 8.57

What is the CGPA to percentage formula?

The most commonly accepted formula in India (per CBSE and many universities): Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. For VTU: (CGPA − 0.75) × 10. For Anna University: CGPA × 10 − 7.5. Always check your university's official circular.

Is 7.5 CGPA good for placements?

Yes. Most top companies like TCS, Infosys, and Wipro have a minimum cutoff of 6.0–6.5 CGPA. Premium companies and PSUs may require 7.5+ or 8.0+. A 7.5 CGPA is generally considered good for most IT and core engineering roles.

How to convert 7.5 CGPA to percentage?

Using generic formula: 7.5 × 9.5 = 71.25%. For VTU: (7.5 − 0.75) × 10 = 67.5%. For Anna: 7.5 × 10 − 7.5 = 67.5%. Use the CGPA to Percentage tab above to get the exact value for your university.

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA (Grade Point Average) typically refers to a single semester's performance. CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is the weighted average across all semesters. In India, both are usually on a 10-point scale, while US universities use a 4-point scale.

📚 Educational Content — Deep Dive

The CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the standard academic grading system used by universities affiliated with AICTE, UGC, and the CBSE board across India. Unlike percentage-based systems, CGPA on a 10-point scale provides a more holistic measure of academic performance by weighting grades against credit hours.
