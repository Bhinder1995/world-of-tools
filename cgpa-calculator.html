What is CGPA and How is it Calculated?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the weighted average of grade points earned across all semesters of a degree program. It's the primary measure of academic performance in Indian universities and colleges. Most Indian universities use a 10-point grading scale where a higher CGPA indicates better performance.

The formula is: CGPA = Σ(Grade Points × Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours. This calculator handles the math for you instantly — just enter your subject grades and credits.

CGPA to Percentage Conversion Formulas for All Universities

Different Indian universities use different formulas to convert CGPA to percentage:

  • ▸VTU / General: Percentage = CGPA × 10
  • ▸CBSE Class 10: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
  • ▸Mumbai University: Percentage = (CGPA − 0.5) × 10
  • ▸AKTU / UPTU: Percentage = CGPA × 10 − 7.5
  • ▸KTU Kerala: Percentage = CGPA × 10 − 5
  • ▸Delhi University: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5 (approx)

Use the CGPA → % tab above to convert your CGPA for any specific university.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to convert CGPA to percentage for VTU?

For VTU (Visvesvaraya Technological University), multiply your CGPA by 10 to get the percentage. A CGPA of 8.5 = 85%. Use the CGPA → % tab and select VTU for instant conversion.

What is a good CGPA in India?

On a 10-point scale: 9.0–10.0 is Outstanding, 8.0–8.9 is Excellent (First Class with Distinction), 7.0–7.9 is First Class, 6.0–6.9 is Second Class. Most companies consider 6.5+ as acceptable for placement.

How to calculate CGPA from marks?

Convert each subject mark to a grade point using your university's grading table. Multiply each grade point by credit hours. Sum all (grade × credits), then divide by total credits. This calculator does this automatically.

What is the CBSE CGPA to percentage formula?

For CBSE Class 10: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. A CGPA of 9.0 equals 9.0 × 9.5 = 85.5%. This formula is officially provided by CBSE.

Is CGPA and GPA the same thing?

GPA (Grade Point Average) is calculated per semester, while CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is the weighted average of all semester GPAs throughout your academic career. CGPA gives a comprehensive view of overall academic performance.

Can I calculate CGPA for 6 or more subjects?

Yes! Use the "Add Subject" button to add as many subjects as needed. The calculator handles any number of subjects with different credit hours.
