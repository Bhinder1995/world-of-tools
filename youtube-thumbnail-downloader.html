Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What resolution is a YouTube thumbnail?

The maximum resolution thumbnail (maxresdefault) is 1280×720 pixels. Older videos may only have HQ (480×360) or SD (320×180) available.

Can I use downloaded thumbnails commercially?

Thumbnails are copyrighted by their creators. Use downloaded thumbnails only for personal reference, design mockups, and educational purposes. Always respect copyright and YouTube's Terms of Service.

Why doesn't the HD thumbnail load for some videos?

Not all videos have a maxresdefault thumbnail. Try HQ or MQ as fallbacks.

Does this work for YouTube Shorts?

Yes — YouTube Shorts use the same video ID system and thumbnails can be downloaded the same way.
