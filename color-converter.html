Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between HEX, RGB, and HSL?

HEX is a base-16 representation of RGB. RGB (Red, Green, Blue) is additive color based on light. HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness) is more intuitive for humans to adjust colors based on how we perceive them.

Can I use this for CSS development?

Yes! The codes generated are standard CSS formats. You can copy the RGB or HSL values directly into your stylesheets.

Does it support transparency (RGBA/HSLA)?

Currently, this tool focuses on opaque colors (HEX, RGB, HSL). We are working on adding alpha channel support for RGBA and HSLA soon.
