How to Use the Regex Tester

  1. Enter Your Pattern: Type your regular expression in the slash-surrounded input field. Do not include the slashes themselves.
  2. Set Flags: Add flags like g (global), i (case-insensitive), or m (multiline) in the small box to the right.
  3. Provide Test Text: Paste or type the text you want to test against in the large text area.
  4. Debug Matches: Look at the highlighted text to see exactly what parts of your string match the pattern. Check the "Match Results" sidebar for detailed indices.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a Regular Expression?

A regular expression (regex) is a sequence of characters that forms a search pattern. It's used for string matching, search-and-replace, and data validation.

Which regex flags should I use?

Common flags include g for all matches, i for ignoring case, and m for multi-line matching where ^ and $ match start/end of lines.
