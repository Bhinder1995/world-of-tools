Why PPF is India's Favorite Tax-Saver

The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is a sovereign-backed scheme that offers Exempt-Exempt-Exempt (EEE) status: No tax on investment, no tax on interest, and no tax on maturity.

🔒 Mandatory Lock-in

15-year initial tenure to encourage long-term wealth building. Extendable indefinitely in blocks of 5 years.

🛡️ Sovereign Safety

Fully backed by the Government of India, making it one of the safest investment avenues available.

Maximizing Your PPF Interest

Interest in PPF is calculated on the minimum balance between the 5th and the last day of the month. To get the best returns, ensure your contribution is deposited before the 5th of every month.

💡 Pro Tip: The April 5th Rule

To earn interest for the full financial year on your entire ₹1.5L investment, deposit the full amount as a lumpsum between April 1st and April 5th. This can lead to significantly higher maturity wealth over 15 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I withdraw money before 15 years?

Partial withdrawals are allowed from the 7th financial year. You can also avail of a loan against your PPF balance between the 3rd and 6th financial years.

Does the interest rate change?

Yes. The Ministry of Finance reviews PPF rates every quarter. It has historically stayed around 7.1% to 8%.

Is my data private?

Yes. Like all tools on WorldOfTools, calculations happen entirely on your device. We do not store or see your financial data.
