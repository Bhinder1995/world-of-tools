Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a limit to how much text I can paste?

No! The tool runs entirely in your browser using your device's memory. You can paste tens of thousands of words instantly without performance issues.

Does it count spaces as characters?

Yes, but it gives you both metrics. Total character count including spaces (essential for Twitter) and a separate count excluding spaces.

Is my text secure and private?

100% Yes. We do not use databases or cloud servers to process your text. The analysis happens locally on your machine.
