Frequently Asked Questions

How many words is a 5-minute speech?

The average speaking pace is 130–150 words per minute. A 5-minute speech is approximately 650–750 words.

How many characters is a tweet (X post)?

X (Twitter) allows 280 characters per tweet for standard users. Use our character counter to ensure you stay within limit.

What is the word limit for a college essay?

Most US college applications (Common App) have a 650-word limit. UK UCAS personal statements have a 4,000-character limit (~600 words).

How many words is a 1,000-word essay?

A 1,000-word essay typically fills 2 pages (double-spaced, Times New Roman 12pt) or about 4 pages single-spaced.

What is average reading time per page?

The average adult reads 200–250 words per minute. A standard 250-word page takes about 1 minute to read.
