What is the Word Counter Tool?

The Word Counter by WorldOfTools is a free, real-time text analysis utility that counts words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and more as you type. Whether you're writing a blog post, an essay, an SEO meta description, a Tweet, or a LinkedIn article — this tool gives you instant precision feedback without any uploads or registration.

All analysis happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never sent to any server, never stored, and never tracked.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I count words online for free?

Paste your text into the box above. The word counter instantly shows word count, character count, sentence count, and reading time — no login required.

What is the word limit for a blog post?

SEO-friendly blog posts are typically 1,200–2,500 words. Long-form content (2,500–4,000 words) tends to rank better for competitive keywords. Use the Goal Tracker above to monitor your progress.

How many words per minute do people read?

The average adult reads approximately 200–250 words per minute silently. This tool uses 225 WPM as the default reading speed estimate. Speaking speed is estimated at 130 WPM for presentations.

Does this tool store my text?

No. All analysis happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never uploaded to any server, stored in any database, or shared with anyone.

How do I check character count for Twitter or Instagram?

Paste your text above. The Platform Limit Checker at the bottom shows instantly if you're within Twitter's 280-character limit, LinkedIn's 3,000-character limit, SMS 160-char limit, or SEO meta description 160-char limit.

What is keyword density and why does it matter?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a specific word appears in your text relative to the total word count. For SEO, keeping your primary keyword density between 1–3% is generally recommended to avoid over-optimization penalties.
