Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is there a word limit for this counter?

No, WorldOfTools allows you to analyze text of any length. Whether it's a short social media post or a long essay, our tool handles it instantly.

Does it count spaces as characters?

We provide both! You can see the total character count (including spaces) and a specific count excluding spaces for more detailed analysis.

Is my text saved on your servers?

Never. All text processing happens locally in your browser. Your content is 100% private and is never uploaded anywhere.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute, the average adult reading speed. This is a reliable approximation for planning blog posts, articles, and presentations.
