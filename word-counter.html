How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or Paste your text into the large box above.
  2. The Counters (Words, Characters, Sentences) will update instantly.
  3. Use the Toolbar Buttons to change case (UPPER/lower) or copy/clear text.
  4. Check the bottom for estimated Reading Time and characters without spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does it count spaces as characters?

We show you both! The main "Characters" count includes spaces, but we also display "Characters (no spaces)" below the text box for your convenience.

Is my text saved or sent to a server?

No, absolutely not. All analysis happens 100% in your browser. Your text never leaves your device, ensuring complete privacy.

How is reading time calculated?

We estimate reading time based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute.
