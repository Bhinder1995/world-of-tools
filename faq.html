Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about WorldOfTools, privacy, and how to use our utilities.

General
What is WorldOfTools?
WorldOfTools is a comprehensive suite of free online utility tools designed specifically for speed, privacy, and ease of use. We offer over 70+ tools including financial calculators, developer utilities, text analyzers, and image optimizers.
Are these tools really free?
Yes, 100% free. There are no hidden charges, no premium tiers, and no "pro" versions. Every tool on WorldOfTools is available for everyone to use at no cost.
Do I need to create an account?
No. You can use all our tools anonymously. We do not require any sign-up, login, or personal information to access our utilities.
Privacy & Security
Is my data safe on WorldOfTools?
Yes. Privacy is our core mission. Most of our tools are built using client-side technology. This means the calculation or processing happens in your browser's memory, not on our servers. Your bank statements, passwords, and private code never leave your device.
How do you monetize if everything is free?
We maintain the site through non-intrusive advertisements and small sponsorships. We never sell user data—partly because we don't even collect it in the first place!
Is WorldOfTools compliant with the DPDP Act?
Yes. We follow the principles of the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act by practicing "Data Minimization." Since we don't upload or store your personal data for most tools, we ensure the highest level of compliance and security for Indian users.
Tool Specifics
How accurate is the GST Calculator?
Our GST Calculator follows the latest GST Council slabs (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and provides accurate CGST, SGST, and IGST breakdowns based on standard Indian tax laws.
Is the Bank Statement Analyzer safe for my financial data?
Absolutely. The Bank Statement Analyzer uses the pdf.js library to parse your PDF locally. Your financial transactions are analyzed in your browser and are never sent to a server. You can even use it while offline after the page loads!
Does the Age Calculator handle leap years?
Yes, our Age Calculator uses precise date-diff algorithms that account for leap years and varying month lengths to give you an age accurate to the second.
What CGPA to Percentage formula do you use?
By default, we use the standard CBSE/University formula (CGPA × 9.5). However, our CGPA Calculator allows you to adjust the multiplier if your specific university uses a different scale (like 10.0).
Does the Image Compressor reduce quality?
Our Image Compressor uses "lossy" compression designed to reduce file size significantly while maintaining "perceptual quality"—meaning the image looks identical to the human eye but takes up much less space.
Technical
Are these tools available offline?
Yes! Thanks to Service Worker technology, many of our tools can work offline once you've visited the page once. This is perfect for developers or travelers with spotty internet.
Can I use these tools on my mobile?
Yes. WorldOfTools is fully responsive and mobile-optimized. You can use any tool on your Android, iPhone, or Tablet just as easily as on a desktop.
Can I suggest a new tool?
We love suggestions! If there's a utility you need that we don't have, please reach out via our Contact Page.
What is "Neo-Brutalism" in your design?
Neo-Brutalism is a modern design trend that uses high contrast, bold borders (strokes), vibrant colors, and unique typography. We use it to make our tools feel "alive," tactile, and easy to navigate.
Is WorldOfTools made in India?
Yes! WorldOfTools is a "Made in India" initiative aimed at providing world-class utilities specifically optimized for the Indian market, including our GST, SIP, and Indian-format Regex tools.
How do I use the JWT Decoder safely?
Since our JWT Decoder is client-side, you can safely paste your tokens without worrying about them being intercepted by a server. It's the safest way for developers to inspect payloads.
Can I export results to Excel or PDF?
Yes, most of our financial and data tools (like the Invoice Generator and Bank Analyzer) offer one-click exports to CSV, Excel, or PDF formats.
How secure is the Password Generator?
Our Password Generator uses the crypto.getRandomValues() API, which is cryptographically secure and much more random than standard math-based generators.
How often do you update the tools?
We update our financial calculators (like PPF and GST) whenever new government rates or slabs are announced. Our developer tools are updated whenever new standards (like a new JWT algorithm) emerge.
Can I use your tools for commercial purposes?
Yes. Freelancers, agencies, and businesses are free to use our tools (like the Invoice Generator or Text Compare) for their professional work.
How do I report a bug?
If something isn't working as expected, please visit our Contact Page and describe the issue. We usually fix reported bugs within 48 hours.
Do you have a dark mode?
We are currently working on a system-wide dark mode. Stay tuned for future updates!
Can I share my calculation results?
Yes, most tools have a "Share" or "Copy link" button that lets you share your specific calculation results with others via WhatsApp or Email.
What is the 'Privacy-First' technology?
It means "Processing at the Edge." Instead of sending your data to our server to get a result, we send the *code* to your browser, which then does the work locally. Your data never moves.
Why does the site load so fast?
We avoid heavy frameworks and bloated libraries. Our site is built with vanilla HTML/CSS and optimized JavaScript, ensuring sub-1-second load times even on mobile networks.
