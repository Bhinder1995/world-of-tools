Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 22, 2026

🛡️ SUMMARY: We don't want your data. Most of our tools process data locally in your browser and never send it to our servers.

1. Information We Collect

At WorldOfTools, we practice Data Minimization. We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share your name to use our utilities. For 90% of our tools (Calculators, Dev Tools, Text Tools), we collect zero personal data.

2. Local Browser Processing

Our "Privacy-First" tools use client-side JavaScript. This means that when you upload a bank statement, paste a JSON string, or generate a password, the work is done by your computer, not our server. Your sensitive inputs are never transmitted, stored, or logged by us.

3. DPDP Act Compliance (India)

We are committed to the principles of the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. We ensure:

  • Purpose Limitation: We only process data for the immediate calculation you requested.
  • Storage Limitation: We do not store your private tool inputs.
  • Transparency: We are clear about which tools are client-side and which are server-side (like URL Shorteners).

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use standard, privacy-compliant analytics (like Google Analytics with anonymized IPs) to understand site traffic. This helps us see which tools are popular so we can improve them. We do not use tracking cookies to build personal profiles of our users.

5. Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their policies.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@worldoftools.in.
