Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 16, 2026

Welcome to **WorldOfTools**. Your privacy is our highest priority. This policy outlines how we handle data and explains our commitment to security and transparency.

1. Information We Collect

Transparency is key: We do not collect any personal identifiable information (PII) like your name, email, or phone number unless you voluntarily provide it. Most of our tools run entirely in your browser (**client-side**), meaning your data never leaves your device.

2. How We Use Data

We use anonymous, non-personal data to monitor usage patterns and improve our toolset. This help us understand which tools are most popular and where we can optimize performance.

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain site performance and some functional features. These are standard identifiers stored on your device to help us recognize repeat visitors and provide a seamless experience.

4. Third-Party Services

Our site uses Google Analytics and AdSense. These services may collect information about your interaction with our site. We ensure these partners adhere to strict privacy standards.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the integrity of our platform. However, since many of our tools process data locally, you have ultimate control over your information security.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this policy, please reach out to our team at **support@worldoftools.in**.
