How it Works

Our QR Code Generator works entirely in your browser. When you type or paste data, our JavaScript library generates the QR code locally on your device. This ensures your data—whether it's an email address, a phone number, or a private message—never touches our servers.

  1. Select Type: Choose the type of QR code you want (URL, WhatsApp, Email, or Phone).
  2. Enter Details: Fill in the required fields. For WhatsApp, include your country code without the '+' or '00'.
  3. Instant Preview: The QR code updates automatically as you type.
  4. Download & Use: Download your QR code as a high-quality PNG for web use, or an SVG for professional printing and resizing.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this QR code generator free and safe?

Yes! It is 100% free and safe. Because all processing happens client-side, your information is never transmitted or stored, making it the most private way to create QR codes.

Why use SVG for QR codes?

SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) allows you to resize the QR code to any size—from a business card to a billboard—without losing any quality or sharpness.

Can I change the QR code later?

These are static QR codes. Once generated and printed, the content cannot be changed. If you need to update the link, you would need to generate a new QR code.

Will these QR codes expire?

No, static QR codes never expire. They will work as long as the underlying link or information remains valid.
