What is a QR Code Generator?

The QR Code Generator by WorldOfTools is a free, browser-based tool that creates QR codes for any type of data — website URLs, WiFi credentials, phone numbers, email addresses, SMS messages, and plain text. All QR codes are generated instantly in your browser using the QRCode.js library — no server, no tracking, no uploads.

QR codes (Quick Response codes) can be scanned by any smartphone camera and instantly open a URL, connect to WiFi, start a call, or compose an email. They're used on business cards, product packaging, restaurant menus, event tickets, and everywhere in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a QR code for my website?

Select 'URL / Link', paste your website URL (including https://), choose size and colors, then click Generate and Download PNG. No expiry — the QR code works forever.

How do I make a WiFi QR code?

Select 'WiFi', enter your network name (SSID), password, and security type. Generate and print the QR code. Guests scan it to connect instantly — no typing required.

Do QR codes from this tool expire?

No. QR codes generated here are static — the data is embedded directly in the image. They never expire as long as the underlying URL or content remains valid.

What is error correction in QR codes?

Error correction allows QR codes to still be scanned even if partially damaged or covered. Higher levels (Q, H) allow more damage tolerance but increase the QR code's complexity. For logos or decorative QR codes, use H level.

Can I use these QR codes commercially?

Yes. All QR codes are free for personal and commercial use — no watermarks, no attribution, no licensing fees.

What size QR code should I use for print?

For business cards: 256px minimum. For A4 posters: 512px or higher. The larger the print, the larger the QR code should be to ensure reliable scanning from a distance.
