How it Works

Our QR Code Generator is completely client-side. We use a high-performance JavaScript library to encode your data into a QR pattern locally in your browser. This means your private links, phone numbers, or emails are never sent to any server.

  1. Choose Template: Select from URL, WhatsApp, Email, or Phone templates.
  2. Fill Details: Input your information accurately.
  3. Automatic Creation: The QR code pattern shifts in real-time.
  4. High-Quality Export: Click download to get a crisp PNG file optimized for printing and digital sharing.
