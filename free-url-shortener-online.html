What is a URL Shortener?

A URL shortener is a tool that converts long, complex web addresses into short, clean links that are easy to share, type, and remember. Instead of sharing a 200-character URL with query parameters and tracking codes, you get a compact link like tinyurl.com/abc123.

Short URLs are essential for Instagram bios (limited character space), printed materials (QR codes and typed links), social media (clean appearance, character limits), and email campaigns (avoid spam filters triggered by long URLs).

How to Shorten a URL — Step by Step

1

Paste Your URL — Copy your long URL and paste it into the input box above. The tool accepts any valid web address starting with https:// or http://.

2

Click Shorten — Press the Shorten button or hit Enter. The short link is generated in under 2 seconds.

3

Copy & Share — Click "Copy Link" to copy the short URL to your clipboard. The QR code is also auto-generated and ready to download.
