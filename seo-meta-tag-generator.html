Meta Tag Best Practices: FAQ

            

                

                    
Why are meta tags important for SEO in 2026?
                    

                    
Meta tags help search engines understand
                        the content of your page. A well-written title and description can significantly improve your
                        click-through rate (CTR) from search results, which is a key ranking signal.

                

                

                    
Is this social media tag compatible?

                    
Yes! We generate Open Graph tags for
                        Facebook and Twitter Cards to ensure your links look professional when shared on social
                        platforms.

                

                

                    
What is a canonical URL?

                    
A canonical URL tells search engines which
                        version of a URL is the "master" one, preventing duplicate content issues that can hurt your
                        rankings.

                

                

                    
What length should my meta description be?
                    

                    
Google typically shows 155-160 characters
                        of your meta description. Keep it under 155 characters and make it compelling to maximize your
                        click-through rate.
