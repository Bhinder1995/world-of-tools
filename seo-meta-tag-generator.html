Why Meta Tags Matter for SEO

Meta tags are invisible HTML instructions that tell search engines and social platforms how to understand and present your content. While only some meta tags directly affect rankings, they all affect click-through rates (CTR) — and CTR is a key ranking signal.

A well-crafted meta title and description can dramatically increase the percentage of searchers who click your result over competitors. Open Graph tags ensure that when someone shares your page on Facebook or LinkedIn, it appears as an attractive card with your chosen image and text rather than a plain URL.

Missing or poorly written meta tags are a fundamental technical SEO mistake. Every public page on your site should have a unique, keyword-rich title and description.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal meta title length?

Google displays approximately 50–60 characters of a title tag. Titles beyond 60 characters get truncated with "..." in search results. Put your primary keyword near the beginning, and include your brand name at the end (separated by a dash or pipe).

Does the meta keywords tag still matter for SEO?

No. Google has ignored the meta keywords tag since 2009. Bing and Yahoo also give it minimal weight. However, it can still provide context for internal site search and some minor search engines. It doesn't hurt to include relevant keywords, but don't expect ranking benefit.

What is the robots meta tag?

The robots meta tag tells search engine crawlers whether to index a page (index/noindex) and whether to follow its outbound links (follow/nofollow). Use noindex for pages like thank-you pages, login pages, and admin sections that you don't want appearing in search results.

What size should my OG image be?

The recommended size for Open Graph images is 1200×630 pixels (roughly 1.91:1 aspect ratio). This looks great on Facebook, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp. For Twitter's large card, the same 1200×630px image works well. Always host the image on a public URL with HTTPS.

Where do I paste the generated meta tags?

Paste the copied tags inside the <head></head> section of your HTML page, typically after the <meta charset> and <meta viewport> tags. In WordPress, use plugins like Yoast SEO or RankMath. In Shopify or Squarespace, use the SEO field in page settings.
