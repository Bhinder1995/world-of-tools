      Frequently Asked Questions
     

     

      
       What is an ideal meta title length?
      
      

       50–60 characters. Google typically truncates titles beyond 60 characters in search results, replacing the rest with "...".
      

     

     

      
       What is an ideal meta description length?
      
      

       150–160 characters. Longer descriptions are cut off in SERPs. Shorter ones may be replaced by Google with content from the page.
      

     

     

      
       Do meta tags affect Google rankings?
      
      

       Meta titles have some SEO ranking influence. Meta descriptions don't directly impact rankings, but they heavily influence **click-through rate (CTR)** from search results.
      

     

     

      
       What is an Open Graph tag?
      
      

       OG tags are HTML meta tags that control how your page appears when shared on social media platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp. They define the title, description, and image shown in link previews.
      

     

     

      
       What size should the OG image be?
      
      

       Recommended: **1200 × 630 pixels** at 72dpi. Minimum: 600 × 315 pixels. Use JPG or PNG format.
      

     

     

      
       Does this tool fetch or analyze my live website?
      
      

       No. It generates tags based only on what you enter — it does not crawl or access your live website.
      

     

     

      
       Are my URLs and page content stored?
      
      

       No. Everything runs in your browser for complete privacy.
      

     

     

      
       Can I generate meta tags for multiple pages?
      
      

       Yes — simply change the inputs for each page and copy the generated code.
