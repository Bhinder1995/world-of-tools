Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

            

                

                    
Why are meta tags important for SEO?

                    
Meta tags help search engines understand
                        the content of your page. A well-written title and description can also significantly improve
                        your click-through rate (CTR) from search results.

                

                

                    
Is this social media tag compatible?

                    
Yes! We generate Open Graph tags for
                        Facebook and Twitter Cards to ensure your links look professional when shared on social
                        platforms.

                

                

                    
What is a canonical URL?

                    
A canonical URL tells search engines which
                        version of a URL is the "master" one, preventing duplicate content issues that can hurt your
                        rankings.
