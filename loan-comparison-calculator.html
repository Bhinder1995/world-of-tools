How to Use the Loan Comparison Calculator

1

Enter Loan Details — Input the principal amount, annual interest rate, and tenure for each loan offer. You can compare up to 3 bank offers side-by-side.

2

Include All Fees — Don't forget processing fees. A 0.5%–2% fee can significantly change the "Effective Interest Rate" of your loan.

3

Spot the Winner — Our tool automatically highlights the most cost-effective deal in green, showing you exactly which bank saves you the most money.

💡 Pro Tip: The Power of Tenure

While a 20-year home loan has a lower EMI than a 15-year one, you will pay significantly more in total interest. Always compare "Total Payment" to see exactly how much extra interest the longer tenure adds to your burden.

📉 Lower EMI ≠ Lower Cost

Banks often push longer tenures to lower your monthly EMI, but this is how they maximize their interest income. Choose the shortest tenure you can comfortably afford.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a 0.5% rate difference really matter?

Absolutely. On a ₹50L home loan for 20 years, a 0.5% lower rate can save you approximately ₹4,00,000 to ₹5,00,000 in interest over the full tenure.

Can I compare different types of loans?

Yes. You can compare a personal loan against a top-up loan, or even compare two different home loan tenures to see which helps you save more.

What if my processing fee is waived?

Simply enter "0" in the Processing Fee field. This allows you to compare a loan with a lower rate but higher fees against one with no fees but a slightly higher rate.
