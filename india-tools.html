Frequently Asked Questions

Which online calculator is best for India?

WorldOfTools offers calculators built specifically for Indian users — GST with CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown, EMI following RBI-standard formula, PPF at the current 7.1% interest rate, CGPA conversion for Indian 10-point grading systems, and GST-inclusive invoice calculations. All completely free.

How accurate is the GST calculator?

The GST calculator follows India's GST council tax slabs (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and uses the standard formula for both inclusive and exclusive GST calculation. It shows CGST and SGST (for intra-state) and IGST (for inter-state) breakdowns as per Indian GST rules.

Is the EMI calculator formula correct?

Yes. The EMI is calculated using the standard reducing balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate, and n is number of months — the same formula used by Indian banks following RBI guidelines.

Can I use these calculators offline?

Once the page is loaded, all calculations work offline because they run in your browser's local JavaScript engine. You can even save the page for offline use.

Is my financial data secure on these tools?

Yes. Every calculation runs entirely in your browser. Your salary, loan amounts, SIP amounts, BMI data and any other values are never uploaded to any server, never stored, and never shared with anyone.
