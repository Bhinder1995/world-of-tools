What is a Loan Eligibility Calculator?

The Loan Eligibility Calculator by WorldOfTools instantly estimates the maximum home loan or personal loan you can get based on your monthly salary, existing EMI obligations, and bank-specific FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) limits.

Indian banks typically allow your total EMI obligations to be between 40–60% of your net monthly income. This calculator shows your maximum eligible EMI, the loan amount this translates to, and how your eligibility compares across major banks like SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, and Kotak.

How to Check Your Loan Eligibility — Step by Step

1

Frequently Asked Questions

How much home loan can I get on ₹50,000 salary?

With ₹50,000 net salary and no existing EMIs, at 50% FOIR your max EMI is ₹25,000/month. At 8.5% p.a. for 20 years, this gives approximately ₹24–₹25 lakh loan. Use this calculator for your exact figure.

What is FOIR in loan eligibility?

FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) is the maximum percentage of your income banks allow for total monthly EMI obligations. Most banks set this at 40–60%. Higher income or better credit score may unlock higher FOIR.

Does existing EMI reduce my eligibility?

Yes. Your existing EMIs reduce the available EMI budget for the new loan. For example, if your income allows ₹30,000 EMI and you already pay ₹10,000 EMI, you can only service ₹20,000 new EMI.

Can both spouses' incomes be combined?

Yes. Most banks allow joint home loans where both spouses' incomes are combined. Add co-applicant income in the calculator to see the joint eligibility.

What is the minimum salary for a home loan?

Most banks require a minimum net monthly salary of ₹25,000–₹30,000 for home loans. For personal loans, some banks approve from ₹15,000/month. PSU banks like SBI may have different thresholds.

Is this calculation accurate for all banks?

This uses standard FOIR-based calculation used by most Indian banks. Actual eligibility may vary based on credit score, employer type, property location, and bank-specific policies. Use this as a reliable estimate.
