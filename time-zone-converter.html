Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between UTC and GMT?

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the global time standard. GMT (Greenwich Mean Time) is a time zone that matches UTC+0 in winter. For most purposes, they're interchangeable — UTC is the modern standard.

How do I convert IST to EST?

IST (India Standard Time) is UTC+5:30. EST (Eastern Standard Time) is UTC-5. IST is 10.5 hours ahead of EST. Example: 9:00 PM IST = 10:30 AM EST.

What is Daylight Saving Time?

Some regions shift their clocks forward 1 hour in summer to make better use of daylight. This changes the UTC offset for those regions seasonally. This tool accounts for DST automatically.

What is the best time for a meeting between India and the US?

Overlapping business hours: 8:00 PM–10:00 PM IST = 7:30 AM–9:30 AM EST (New York). Use this converter to find the exact window for your specific dates.

Does this work for historical time zone conversions?

Yes — enter any past date and the converter applies the historically correct UTC offset and DST rules for that date.
