Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed amount paid every month to repay a loan. It includes both the principal repayment and the interest component.

How is home loan EMI calculated?

Using the formula: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N − 1], where P is the loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the tenure in months.

What is the EMI for a ₹20 lakh home loan?

At 8.5% interest for 20 years: approximately ₹17,356 per month. The exact amount depends on your actual rate and tenure.

Is it better to choose a short or long loan tenure?

Short tenure = higher EMI but lower total interest. Long tenure = lower EMI but you pay significantly more interest overall. Use this calculator to compare both options.

Does prepayment reduce EMI?

Yes — partial prepayment either reduces your remaining tenure or reduces future EMI amounts, depending on your lender's policy.

What happens if I miss an EMI payment?

Missing EMI payments incurs late payment fees, increases total interest, and negatively impacts your CIBIL credit score.

Is this EMI calculator accurate?

Yes. It uses the same formula used by banks, NBFCs, and financial institutions. Results are highly accurate for fixed-rate loans.

Does it calculate floating rate EMI?

The calculator is best for fixed-rate loans. For floating rate loans, calculate at both optimistic (lower) and pessimistic (higher) rate scenarios for a range estimate.
