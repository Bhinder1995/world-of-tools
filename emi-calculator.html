Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is an EMI?

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. It includes both interest and principal components.

Can I use this for a Car Loan EMI?

Absolutely! This calculator works for any reducing balance loan, including Car Loans, Home Loans, and Personal Loans.

How is the EMI calculated?

Our calculator uses the standard formula [P x R x (1+R)^N]/[(1+R)^N-1], where P is Principal, R is monthly interest rate, and N is the number of installments.

What is a good EMI-to-income ratio?

Financial advisors generally recommend keeping your total EMI obligations below 40% of your net monthly income to maintain a healthy budget and good credit score.
