Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is a fixed amount paid by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs are used to pay off both interest and principal each month.

How does tenure affect my EMI?

A longer tenure (more years) reduces your monthly EMI amount but increases the total interest you pay over the life of the loan. A shorter tenure increases your EMI but saves you money on interest.

Can I use this for Home and Car loans?

Yes, this calculator works for any loan that follows a standard reducing balance method, including Home Loans, Car Loans, and Personal Loans.
