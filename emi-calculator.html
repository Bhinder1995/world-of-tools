What is an EMI?

An EMI is a fixed amount of money that a borrower pays to a lender at a specific date each month. It consists of two components:

  • Principal Component: The actual loan amount that is being paid back.
  • Interest Component: The cost of borrowing the money, calculated on the reducing balance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a longer tenure decrease the total interest?

No, a longer tenure decreases your monthly EMI but significantly **increases** the total interest you pay over the life of the loan. Use our calculator to find the right balance for your finances.

What is a reducing balance rate?

Most bank loans use a reducing balance method where interest is calculated only on the remaining principal amount each month, rather than the initial loan amount.
