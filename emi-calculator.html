How EMI is Calculated

📊 The Mathematical Formula

Our tool uses the standard Reducing Balance Method formula: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N − 1]. P is your principal, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the number of months. This ensures you see the most accurate monthly cost used by banks worldwide.

💡 Understanding the Visual Breakdown

The Pie Chart and Growth Bar show you the ratio of your Principal vs. Interest. In early years of a loan, a larger portion of your EMI goes toward interest. As the loan progresses, more of your payment goes toward the actual principal—our visualizer makes this trend easy to understand.

🏠 Home vs. Personal Loans

While the formula remains the same, Home Loans typically have lower interest rates and longer tenures (up to 30 years), while Personal Loans have higher rates and shorter tenures (1-5 years). Use the chart to see how changing the tenure significantly impacts your total interest cost.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How is my monthly EMI determined?

EMI is based on three factors: the loan amount, the annual interest rate, and the repayment period. Our calculator computes this instantly and shows the breakdown in a pie chart.

What does the "Principal vs Interest" chart show?

It shows the total amount you will pay back. The "Principal" is the original money you borrowed, and the "Interest" is the extra cost of borrowing that money over time.

Does increasing tenure reduce EMI?

Yes, a longer tenure reduces your monthly payment but massively increases the total interest you pay. Use the visual growth bar to see how a 20-year loan costs much more than a 15-year one.

Is this tool private?

Absolutely. Your financial data never leaves your browser. We use JavaScript for local processing, ensuring 100% data security and privacy.
